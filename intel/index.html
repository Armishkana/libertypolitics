Add to the Intel Room

Found something that belongs here, or made it yourself? Send the link. I check it, and it goes on the map with your name. +40 XP, +90 XP for your own work, which everyone who uses the room sees, and so do the audiences of the streams that show it.

How the Intel Room grows

  1. 1You send a link. A clip, a post, a document, a number or today's news, or your own video or article. One line on what it shows.
  2. 2I check it. That it opens, that it shows what it says, where and when. Usually within a day.
  3. 3It goes on the map with your name. Anyone can find it, use it on a show or send it to the person who needs to see it. You get the XP.

Smotrich, on the record

11 things Smotrich has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes in Congress. A line that does not settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • June 15, 2026 · SpeechIran: the regime

    At his party's weekly meeting he opposed the agreement between the United States and Iran and said Israel would keep working to bring down the government in Tehran.

    “The agreement with Iran is a bad agreement for Israel and for the entire free world. Iran must not have nuclear weapons, and we must not accept a reality in which the extremist regime in Tehran continues advancing toward that goal.”
    By the test we use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Opposing a deal with Tehran and demanding it never get nuclear weapons is pressure on the Islamic Republic.

  • October 26, 2025 · SpeechMilitary

    At a tax advisers' conference he said the defence budget could not stay at its wartime level and had to come back down.

    “While the budget will remain higher than it was before the war, it cannot continue at the levels of the past two years, during which it essentially received a blank check. We must return to normal figures.”
    By the test we use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Calling for the defence budget to be brought down is on the cut-defence side of the measure.

    With the prime minister he announced that the 2026 budget would be passed at once with a larger defence budget at its centre; the government approved an increase of about 30 billion shekels that night.

    “we are now moving toward the immediate passage of the state budget, at the center of which will be the required increase to the defense budget to finance the war”
    By the test we use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Raising the defence budget is funding and equipping the armed forces.

    By the test we use for votes

    FAgainst liberty

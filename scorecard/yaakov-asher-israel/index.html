Asher, on the record

11 things Asher has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • September 29, 2025 · VoteMilitary

    Degel HaTorah's Knesset members, the faction Asher sits in, voted against the bill that added about 31 billion shekels to the 2025 state budget for defence costs, on the instruction of Rabbi Dov Landau; Asher answered the finance minister's criticism of that instruction.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    A vote against extra money for the armed forces in wartime is a vote against funding them.

  • July 2, 2026 · StatementMilitary

    In the Knesset Foreign Affairs and Defense Committee, Asher objected to treating yeshiva students who do not report for army service as draft evaders, saying their number had grown from 15,000 to 90,000.

    “These are law-abiding citizens whose only sin is studying Torah. In a single day, you turned 90,000 people into lawbreakers.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    He opposes enforcing conscription on tens of thousands of men the army has called up.

    Source: JFeed, MK Yaakov Asher erupts: "You've turned 90,000 Haredim into criminals"Discuss this
  • March 14, 2024 · SpeechBorders

    At an event marking the arrival of foreign construction workers from India and Sri Lanka, Asher said he had asked for the bilateral agreements to be set aside because they take a long time, and that he wanted to see 10,000 workers arrive.

    “I will be happier when I see 10,000 workers, and I assume we will see that quota in the coming weeks”

    In Hebrew: אשמח יותר כשאראה 10,000 עובדים ואני מניח שנראה בשבועות הקרובים את המכסה הזאת

    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Temporary work permits for a set number of foreign builders are not settlement or amnesty, so the line does not settle the measure.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, The government in an effort to bring workers to the construction sectorDiscuss this
  • January 28, 2025 · StatementBorders

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

  • March 22, 2026 · StatementSecularism

    In a Knesset Interior Committee exchange about 5 billion shekels of coalition funds, Asher defended the money as education budgets for certain communities that his side had fought for.

    “These coalition funds are part of the budget that the State of Israel allocates to certain communities for education. This is exactly something we have fought for over the years”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Coalition funds are state money shared out under the agreements that hold a coalition together, here for his community's schools.

    Source: Matzav, Heated Knesset Clash Over Budget Priorities: Lahav-Hertzanu and Yaakov Asher Trade BlowsDiscuss this

    After Yair Lapid wrote that the next government would let people marry and divorce in civil courts, Asher answered on X that this would allow a million Belarusians to be brought in and married.

    “And then it will be possible to bring a million Belarusians, as Ehud Barak proposed, and they will be able to marry”

    In Hebrew: ואז יהיה אפשר: להביא מיליון בלארוסים, כמו שהציע אהוד ברק, הם יוכלו להתחתן

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    He argues against civil marriage, which would end the religious monopoly on marriage.

    Source: Kikar HaShabbat, "A million times prefer Russian speakers": the Haredi Knesset member in a tough clash with Yair LapidDiscuss this

    Asher was one of the sponsors of the Religious Courts Arbitration law, passed by the Knesset 65 to 41, which lets rabbinic and Sharia courts act as arbitrators in civil disputes when all sides agree.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    The law he sponsored widens what religious courts, rabbinic and Sharia, may decide between citizens.

  • September 28, 2026 · StatementSecularism

    As chairman of United Torah Judaism, Asher said the party will not support or join any government unless the status of yeshiva students is settled in law.

    “The only thing that guides us is the instruction of the great rabbis of Israel, under which United Torah Judaism, in all its parts, will not support and will not enter any government without the status of Torah learners being settled.”

    In Hebrew: הדבר היחיד שמנחה אותנו זו הוראת גדולי ישראל, לפיה יהדות התורה על כל מרכיביה לא תתמוך ולא תיכנס לאף ממשלה ללא הסדרת מעמדם של לומדי התורה

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    He makes a religious exemption in law the price of his party's support for a government.

    Source: Kikar HaShabbat, No government without a draft law: the declared red line of United Torah JudaismDiscuss this
  • August 6, 2024 · SpeechCapitalism

    Asher submitted a bill to make mortgage advisers a regulated profession, with entry exams and a status that obliges banks to work with them.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Licensing an occupation by law and obliging banks to deal with it is more regulation.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, MK Asher: on the way to regulating the status of mortgage advisersDiscuss this

    “We are taking a significant step in the fight against cumbersome bureaucracy”

    In Hebrew: אנחנו עושים צעד משמעותי במאבק נגד הבירוקרטיה המסורבלת

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He removed a permit requirement, which is less regulation.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, Approved for first reading: home electricity connections will be enlarged with no need for a building permitDiscuss this

    FAgainst liberty

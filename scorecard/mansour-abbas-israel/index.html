Abbas, on the record

21 things Abbas has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • November 27, 2021 · InterviewTyranny

    Asked at a public event in Beersheba about the dangers Israel faces, he said the Islamic Republic's nuclear programme is a real threat but not an existential one and should be left to the professionals.

    “I don't see the Iranian nuclear issue as an existential threat to Israel. There is certainly a threat, but we'll leave that to the professionals”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    He calls the programme a real threat while declining to call it existential, and proposes no policy either way, so the words do not settle the measure.

    Source: Algemeiner (JNS), 'Nuclear Iran not an existential threat to Israel,' says Ra'am leaderDiscuss this
  • October 10, 2023 · StatementTyranny

    Three days after the 7 October attack he called on the leaders of the armed factions in Gaza to release the hostages they held.

    “I call on the leadership of the Palestinian factions in Gaza to release the captives in your hands.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Demanding that terror groups free their hostages is confronting them, not excusing them.

    Source: Israel National News, Arab leaders denounce Hamas conductDiscuss this
  • December 1, 2023 · InterviewTyranny

    Told CNN that armed Palestinian groups must give up their weapons and condemned the 7 October massacre as immoral and against Islam; his party said the next day that the weapons would pass to a Palestinian state once one is established.

    “the palestinian organizations need to unload their weapons.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Condemning a terror attack and calling on the armed groups to disarm confronts them, though his party then tied the disarming to a future state.

    Source: Israel National News, Ra'am chairman's interview and the party's clarificationDiscuss this
  • May 2024 · InterviewTyranny

    Asked on Channel 13 whether Hamas, after 7 October, should be treated as an outcast like ISIS, he answered that Hamas is a faction and part of the Palestinian people.

    “Hamas is a faction.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Declining to set Hamas apart as a terror group after 7 October, and calling it a faction, is refusing to call a terror group one.

    Source: Israel National News, MK Mansour Abbas: 'Hamas is part of the Palestinian people'Discuss this
  • May 22, 2024 · InterviewTyranny

    In a Kan interview he said Hamas had committed crimes and that those who committed crimes should pay the price, while saying he had no opinion on whether Israel had committed crimes.

    “those who committed crimes should pay the price, hamas has committed crimes”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Saying a terror group must pay for its crimes backs holding it to account.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas retracts: 'There are no wars without crimes against civilians'Discuss this
  • July 28, 2026 · InterviewTyranny

    Asked again and again on Kan Reshet Bet radio whether Hamas is a terrorist organisation that should be destroyed, he declined to answer, after saying his party had condemned the crimes of 7 October.

    “I have a straight answer; I just don't want to say it.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Declining, when asked directly, to call a terror army a terror group is refusing to call a terror group one.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas refuses to condemn HamasDiscuss this
  • December 21, 2021 · InterviewFree nations

    Asked at the Globes Israel Business Conference whether he could accept Israel as a Jewish state, he said it was born one and will remain one, and that the question is the status of its Arab citizens.

    “The State of Israel was born as a Jewish state. That's the people's decision and the question is not about the identity of the state. It was born that way and that's how it will remain.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Accepting the country as its people founded it, where other Arab parties demand that it stop being a Jewish state, stands with the democracy rather than against its existence.

    Source: Globes, Mansour Abbas: Israel was born Jewish and will remain soDiscuss this
  • December 25, 2023 · SpeechFree nations

    In a short Arabic speech in the Knesset, eleven weeks after the 7 October attack and with hostages still held in Gaza, he demanded that the war end outright, not only a ceasefire.

    “our demand today is being made clearly and openly, and it is that this war come to an end, not just a ceasefire.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Demanding that the country stop its defense while the terror army that attacked it still holds hostages goes against that defense.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas calls for an end to the warDiscuss this
  • July 17, 2024 · SpeechFree nations

    In a Knesset speech he called for ending the war in Gaza, saying 50,000 civilians had been murdered there; the Speaker ordered him removed from the podium.

    “50,000 civilians who were murdered in the gaza strip”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Calling the country's war against Hamas the murder of civilians, and demanding it end while hostages were still held, goes against the country's defense.

    Source: Israel National News, Ire in the Knesset: MK Mansour Abbas' inciteful statementDiscuss this
  • August 26, 2026 · PostFree nations

    In an Arabic Facebook post during the 2026 campaign he wrote that Israel's Jewish character is a fact forced on Arab citizens, which Arab parties accept because otherwise they could be barred from elections.

    “The Jewish character of the state is an existing fact that was imposed on us, and we did not choose it”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Accepting the state as it is while saying the acceptance was forced neither backs nor opposes the country, so it does not settle the measure.

    Source: Ynetnews, Mansour Abbas says Israel's Jewish identity was 'imposed on us,' accepted to run in electionsDiscuss this

    In the talks to form the Bennett-Lapid government he won a NIS 30 billion five-year state plan for developing the Arab sector, double the previous plan.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Doubling a state spending plan is much more state spending.

    Source: Globes, Mansour Abbas doubles budget for Arab sectorDiscuss this

    The coalition agreement his party signed to join the Bennett-Lapid government, the first time an Arab party joined an Israeli government, commits the government to Arab representation in public offices and state companies in line with their share of the population.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Setting places in public offices by national group is a quota by group.

    Source: Israel National News, UAL agreement: Most illegal Bedouin villages to be recognized by governmentDiscuss this
  • June 2, 2021 · StatementEqual rights

    As a condition for joining the coalition he demanded that the new government's guidelines include no laws in favour of the LGBT community.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Keeping laws for gay people out of a government's programme as a price of support opposes equal rights for gay people under the same law.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas signs, government formedDiscuss this
  • May 16, 2021 · StatementConscience

    During the May 2021 riots he visited a synagogue in Lod burned by Arab rioters, called for calm, and said he would lead the restoration of the synagogues that were harmed.

    “We are willing to take part in restoring the synagogues that were harmed. I will lead it.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Pledging to rebuild another faith's houses of worship after they were burned protects every faith's freedom to worship.

    Source: Algemeiner, Israeli Arab leader Mansour Abbas visits Lod synagogue burned by riotersDiscuss this
  • November 25, 2021 · InterviewConscience

    Told the Kul al-Arab newspaper that the Temple Mount belongs to Muslims only and that his party was working inside the government for a commitment that non-Muslims will not worship there.

    “We are working in the government to make a commitment not to invade the al-Aqsa mosque, not to perform religious worship on it by non-Muslims as it is a Muslim-only right.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Using a place in government to keep people of other faiths from worshipping at a holy site puts one religion's rule into state policy, against equal freedom to worship.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas: Temple Mount belongs only to MuslimsDiscuss this
  • May 30, 2022 · Government actConscience

    Ra'am, which he leads and which sat in the governing coalition, reached an agreement with the finance minister for 35 state posts for Muslim imams in the Interior Ministry.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    State money for religious posts agreed with a party in return for its support is a state favour to religious bodies, against liberty on conscience.

    Source: Israel National News, With Shaked's support: Liberman's agreement with Ra'amDiscuss this
  • May 2026 · InterviewDefense

    On Radio al-Nas, days after saying national service could help Arab society, he said the service he means is voluntary community work independent of the state, and that the rights of Arab citizens do not depend on any service to it.

    “our rights as an arab society, as natives and indigenous people of this country, are not dependent on such conditions”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Rejecting any duty of service to the state for a whole group of citizens keeps that group outside the service the country depends on.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas walks back call for Arab national serviceDiscuss this
  • July 2026 · SpeechDefense

    Said at a national security conference that he backs civic service for Arab citizens in a framework of its own, and told the Arab outlet Bokra it should be voluntary and fully separate from any military or security body.

    “a framework is needed that addresses both the needs of young arab citizens and broader israeli expectations”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Backing a voluntary civilian service while ruling out military or security service neither shares the army's burden nor rejects all service, so it does not settle the measure.

    Source: Israel National News, Mansour Abbas: Yes to civic service for Arabs, no military or security tiesDiscuss this

    The same coalition agreement extends the freeze on enforcement, fines and demolition of illegal buildings until 2024 and grants legal status to most of the unrecognised Bedouin villages in the Negev.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Freezing enforcement of the building law and legalising villages built without permits sets aside the enforcement of the country's own laws.

  • March 1, 2026 · StatementIran: the regime

    After the joint United States and Israeli attack on Iran in which Ali Khamenei was killed, Feijóo said the world is better when a tyrant falls, while also asking for restraint and a negotiated way out.

    “the world is better when a tyrant falls”

    In Spanish: el mundo es mejor cuando cae un tirano

    By the test we use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Welcoming the fall of the Islamic Republic's leader after strikes on the regime, instead of condemning the strikes, counts as with liberty.

  • September 19, 2025 · SpeechIsrael

    At a FAES event Feijóo criticised the government for condemning the Israeli people, whom he described as the only democracy in the Middle East and still waiting for their hostages. In the same speech he spoke of a massacre in Gaza and did not use the word genocide.

    “It is not normal to condemn the Israeli people, who are still waiting for the release of their innocent hostages, or to have to recall that it is the only working democracy in the Middle East.”

    In Spanish: No es normal que se condene al pueblo israelí que todavía espera la liberación de sus rehenes inocentes y que se deba recordar que representa la única democracia vigente en Oriente Medio.

    By the test we use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Defending Israel as a democracy against the government's condemnation counts as with liberty.

    As army chief, six months after the 2015 nuclear deal with Iran, he told a security conference that the deal held many dangers but also opportunities, and that Iran would keep it for five years and return to seeking a bomb within fifteen.

    “The deal is a significant change in the vector Iran is going toward.”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

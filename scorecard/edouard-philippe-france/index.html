Philippe, on the record

14 things Philippe has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes in Congress. A line that does not settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • December 8, 2025 · InterviewIran: the people

    Asked about the Islamic veil, said he has more admiration for the women in Iran who fight to take it off.

    “I have more admiration for the women in Iran who fight to take it off.”

    In French: J'ai plus d'admiration pour les femmes en Iran qui se battent pour le retirer.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Speaking for Iranian women against the rule their rulers impose counts as with liberty.

    Source: TF1 Info, "Objectif 2027" : budget, immigration, narcotrafic... les temps forts d'Édouard Philippe sur LCIDiscuss this
  • December 8, 2025 · InterviewAllies

    Said France may one day have to talk to Putin again, and that it would be better to do so from strength than from weakness.

    “Perhaps one day we will have to talk to Putin again. It would be better to resume the conversation from a position of strength rather than weakness.”

    In French: Peut-être qu'un jour il faudra reparler avec Poutine. Ce serait mieux de reprendre la conversation dans un rapport de force plutôt que de faiblesse.

    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    Being open to talks with Moscow from a position of strength neither sides with Russia nor adds to support for Ukraine.

    Source: TF1 Info, "Objectif 2027" : budget, immigration, narcotrafic... les temps forts d'Édouard Philippe sur LCIDiscuss this

    Travelled to Kyiv as a presidential candidate in May 2026, three days after a mass Russian missile and drone attack, and met President Zelensky.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Going to Kyiv to back Ukraine in his campaign is standing with Ukraine against Russia.

    In French: la voix de la faiblesse et de l'alignement coupable

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Defending aid to Ukraine against a call to end it is standing with Ukraine against Russia.

  • November 5, 2023 · InterviewMilitary

    Said the question of giving military training to part or all of French youth may soon have to be asked.

    “Whether one day, to protect France's interests and its security, we will have to train, and train militarily or it makes no sense, part of the young or even all of the young, is a question that may be asked”

    In French: Est-ce qu'un jour, pour préserver les intérêts de la France, sa sécurité, il ne faudra pas former - et former militairement, sinon ça n'a aucun sens - une partie de la jeunesse, voire toute la jeunesse, c'est une question qui sera peut-être posée

    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    He raises the return of military service as a question without proposing it, so the line does not settle the measure.

    In French: Ceux qui ne respectent pas nos règles n'ont rien à y faire et doivent être expulsés, quitte à imposer un rapport de force avec les États concernés.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Deporting those who break the rules counts as with liberty.

    With liberty

    “There is no reason to let a state of the law continue that would suggest we have a special relationship with Algeria”

    In French: Il n'y a aucune raison de laisser perdurer un état du droit qui laisserait penser qu'on a une relation particulière avec l'Algérie

    With liberty

  • September 2, 2026 · InterviewIslamization

    Said he fights the National Rally's plan to fine women for wearing the Islamic veil in the street, while saying he is not at ease with the veil.

    “I may not be at ease with the wearing of the veil, but that does not mean I want to ban it”

    In French: Je peux ne pas être à l'aise avec le port du voile, mais ça ne veut pas dire que je veux l'interdire

    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

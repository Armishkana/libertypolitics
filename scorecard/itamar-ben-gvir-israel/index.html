Ben-Gvir, on the record

14 things Ben-Gvir has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • November 26, 2024 · VoteTyranny

    He was the only member of the security cabinet to vote against the ceasefire with Hezbollah in Lebanon, saying it would not deter Hezbollah.

    “The cabinet's decision tonight is a serious mistake. A ceasefire at this stage will not return the residents of the north to their homes, will not deter Hezbollah”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Voting against ending pressure on Hezbollah while it is still armed is confronting a terror army.

    Source: Israel National News, After cabinet approval: Lebanon ceasefire takes effectDiscuss this
  • January 19, 2025 · Government actTyranny

    He resigned from the government with his party's other ministers in protest at the Gaza ceasefire and hostage release deal, calling it a capitulation to terror.

    “This dangerous deal that the government, under your leadership, signed is unfortunately a capitulation to terror.”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Bringing captives home is a state's duty, and a deal for them also pays a terror group, so a hostage deal does not settle the measure for anyone, whether they back it or oppose it.

    Source: Ynetnews, Ben-Gvir resigns from government, coalition to return if fighting in Gaza resumesDiscuss this
  • June 15, 2026 · StatementTyranny

    After the United States and Iran announced an agreement, he said as a member of the security cabinet that Israel was not a party to it and was not bound by it.

    “My position is clear: we are not a party to this agreement, which does not look after our security, and it does not bind us in any way.”

    In Hebrew: עמדתי ברורה: איננו שותפים להסכם הזה שאיננו דואג לבטחוננו, והוא איננו מחייב אותנו בשום צורה.

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Opposing a deal with Tehran is on the side of keeping pressure on the Islamic Republic.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, Ben-Gvir on the agreement with Iran: Israel is not bound by the agreement and will not stay silent under fireDiscuss this
  • September 9, 2026 · StatementTyranny

    He and his party asked the Central Elections Committee to bar the Islamic party Ra'am and its chairman Mansour Abbas from the 2026 election, saying Ra'am denies Israel as a Jewish state and that a charity tied to it moved money to terrorist organisations.

    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Ra'am is a legal party in Israel's parliament. Governing with it, or trying to bar it from the ballot, is judged by what the government then does, not by the religion of a partner, so this line does not settle the test.

    Source: The Jerusalem Post, Otzma Yehudit requests Ra'am party, Abbas be blocked from running in electionDiscuss this
  • November 2023 · Government actFree nations

    In the weeks after the 7 October 2023 attack, as minister, he handed out rifles to civilian members of local security squads, and his office said weapons would go to squads across the country.

    “We saw this in the first days of the war, wherever there were weapons, the scale of the disaster was smaller”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Arming local defense squads after the attack is acting as minister for the country's defense.

    Source: CNN (via ABC 17 News), Israelis are arming up in the aftermath of Hamas attackDiscuss this
  • January 2, 2026 · PostFree nations

    During street protests in Iran he published a post in Persian backing the demonstrators and ending with the words "We are with you!"

    “The Iranian people deserve to be free, free of the murderous dictator Khamenei.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He spoke up for Iranian protesters against their rulers.

    Source: Israel National News, 'Death to the dictator': Minister Ben-Gvir's tweet in FarsiDiscuss this
  • March 20, 2023 · DecreeFree speech

    He signed an order closing the Jerusalem broadcasting centre of Voice of Palestine, the Palestinian Authority's official radio station, and banning its activity anywhere in Israel, accusing it of incitement.

    “I will not accept and we will not allow incitement and support for terrorism and terrorists, neither by the Palestinian Authority nor by any other body.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    A minister's order shutting a broadcaster is against free speech whoever the target is.

    Source: Israel National News, National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir orders closure of Voice of Palestine radioDiscuss this
  • June 13, 2023 · StatementFree speech

    He wrote to the police commissioner and the Tel Aviv district commander that the arrest of a Haredi man who held a sign reading "I protest" near the Tel Aviv pride parade broke the law and his own policy.

    “This is a violation of the law and a violation of freedom of expression.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He objected to police arresting a man for a lawful protest sign.

    Source: Israel National News, Ben-Gvir accuses police of suppressing free expressionDiscuss this
  • March 21, 2025 · StatementFree speech

    At a police briefing at the Western Wall during Ramadan he praised the police fight against people he called inciters and said there was zero tolerance for PLO flags and for marches he described as supporting terrorism.

    “We have zero tolerance for PLO flags and marches in support of terrorism.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Having police stop a flag and marches because of their message is the state banning speech.

    Source: Israel National News, Minister Ben Gvir at Western Wall: 'We won't tolerate terrorism and incitement'Discuss this
  • December 5, 2025 · StatementFree markets

    After long talks with the finance ministry his ministry won 4.5 billion shekels more for 2026, 3,000 new posts and about 1.5 billion shekels in pay rises for police, prison and fire staff, a deal people close to him called an important achievement.

    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    A bigger police budget is not a tax rise, a price control or state ownership, so the line does not settle the measure.

    Source: Israel National News, Victory for Ben-Gvir: Police, Prison Service, and Fire Services budgets to increaseDiscuss this
  • September 7, 2022 · InterviewConscience

    In an interview before the 2022 election he said marriage in Israel must stay under Jewish religious law and that he would not allow same-sex marriage or surrogacy for same-sex couples in law.

    “My lines are a Jewish state. Marriages must be according to the law of Moses and Israel”

    In Hebrew: הקווים שלי הם מדינה יהודית. נישואים צריכים להיות כדת משה וישראל

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Keeping marriage under religious law and ruling out civil marriage puts a religion's rules into law.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, Ben-Gvir: my red line is LGBT marriageDiscuss this
  • October 28, 2024 · SpeechDefense

    In the dispute over drafting Haredi men he told his party's Knesset members that he believes in full army service but not in forcing people to enlist, and that the first step should be benefits for those who serve.

    “We believe in enlisting in the army, but not by coercion”

    In Hebrew: אנחנו מאמינים בגיוס לצבא, אבל לא בכפייה

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Rejecting compulsion in the draft is opposing conscription the army depends on, whatever his support for service by choice.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, Minister Ben-Gvir: we believe in enlistment, but not by coercionDiscuss this
  • September 22, 2026 · Party programmeDefense

    Presenting his party's demands for the next government, he asked for the defence ministry for himself and set out a plan that includes raising soldiers' pay and giving combat soldiers free land.

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Raising soldiers' pay is on the side of funding the armed forces.

    Source: Israel National News, Ben Gvir seeks to become Defense MinisterDiscuss this
  • December 4, 2025 · StatementBorders

    After ordering more prison cells for people who entered Israel illegally, he visited the prison and told migrants held there to return to their countries or stay locked up.

    “We are adding more room for infiltrators. I think you need to be in prison.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Detaining people who entered illegally and pressing them to leave is enforcing immigration law.

    Source: Israel National News, Ben-Gvir to illegal migrants in prison: 'I care for our girls'Discuss this

Ben-Gvir, on the record

11 things Ben-Gvir has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes in Congress. A line that does not settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • June 15, 2026 · StatementIran: the regime

    After the United States and Iran announced an agreement, he said as a member of the security cabinet that Israel was not a party to it and was not bound by it.

    “My position is clear: we are not a party to this agreement, which does not look after our security, and it does not bind us in any way.”

    In Hebrew: עמדתי ברורה: איננו שותפים להסכם הזה שאיננו דואג לבטחוננו, והוא איננו מחייב אותנו בשום צורה.

    By the test we use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Opposing a deal with Tehran is on the side of keeping pressure on the Islamic Republic.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, Ben-Gvir on the agreement with Iran: Israel is not bound by the agreement and will not stay silent under fireDiscuss this
  • January 2, 2026 · PostIran: the people

    During street protests in Iran he published a post in Persian backing the demonstrators and ending with the words "We are with you!"

    “The Iranian people deserve to be free, free of the murderous dictator Khamenei.”
    By the test we use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He spoke up for Iranian protesters against their rulers.

    Source: Israel National News, 'Death to the dictator': Minister Ben-Gvir's tweet in FarsiDiscuss this
  • October 28, 2024 · SpeechMilitary

    In the dispute over drafting Haredi men he told his party's Knesset members that he believes in full army service but not in forcing people to enlist, and that the first step should be benefits for those who serve.

    “We believe in enlisting in the army, but not by coercion”

    In Hebrew: אנחנו מאמינים בגיוס לצבא, אבל לא בכפייה

    By the test we use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Rejecting compulsion in the draft is opposing conscription the army depends on, whatever his support for service by choice.

    Source: Arutz Sheva, Minister Ben-Gvir: we believe in enlistment, but not by coercionDiscuss this

  • December 4, 2025 · StatementBorders

    After ordering more prison cells for people who entered Israel illegally, he visited the prison and told migrants held there to return to their countries or stay locked up.

    “We are adding more room for infiltrators. I think you need to be in prison.”

    He and his party asked the Central Elections Committee to bar the Islamic party Ra'am and its chairman Mansour Abbas from the 2026 election, saying Ra'am denies Israel as a Jewish state and that a charity tied to it moved money to terrorist organisations.

  • September 7, 2022 · InterviewSecularism

    In an interview before the 2022 election he said marriage in Israel must stay under Jewish religious law and that he would not allow same-sex marriage or surrogacy for same-sex couples in law.

    “My lines are a Jewish state. Marriages must be according to the law of Moses and Israel”

    In Hebrew: הקווים שלי הם מדינה יהודית. נישואים צריכים להיות כדת משה וישראל

    By the test we use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Keeping marriage under religious law and ruling out civil marriage puts a religion's rules into law.

    He signed an order closing the Jerusalem broadcasting centre of Voice of Palestine, the Palestinian Authority's official radio station, and banning its activity anywhere in Israel, accusing it of incitement.

    “I will not accept and we will not allow incitement and support for terrorism and terrorists, neither by the Palestinian Authority nor by any other body.”
    By the test we use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    A minister's order shutting a broadcaster is against free speech whoever the target is.

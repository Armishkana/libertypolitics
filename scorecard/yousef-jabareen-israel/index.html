Jabareen, on the record

11 things Jabareen has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • July 2019 · InterviewIran: the regime

    As a Knesset member he told the British newspaper Express that Israel applies a double standard on nuclear supervision and that talks with Iran, not confrontation, were the way forward.

    “Political negotiations and compromise are the key - any alternative would be a disaster for the entire world”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Arguing for compromise with Tehran in place of sanctions and pressure is opposing pressure on the Islamic Republic.

    Source: Communist Party of Israel, MK Jabareen: Netanyahu Is the Real Winner in the US-Iran ConflictDiscuss this
  • June 8, 2026 · StatementIran: the regime

    After he became its chairman, Hadash issued a statement calling on the government to stop the escalation with Iran and on opposition leaders to oppose a war.

    “No to war with Iran. No to Netanyahu's eternal war.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Opposing military action against the Islamic Republic is the opposite of backing pressure on it.

    Source: Communist Party of Israel, Hadash: No to Netanyahu's Eternal WarDiscuss this
  • July 19, 2026 · StatementMilitary

    Hadash, under his leadership, said in a joint statement with Balad that it absolutely opposes any recruitment or service programme for Arab citizens, military or civilian.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Rejecting every military recruitment programme for a fifth of the country's citizens opposes service the army draws on.

    Source: Israel National News, Hadash and Balad reject all forms of military and civil serviceDiscuss this
  • September 29, 2026 · Party programmeMilitary

    The Joint List, which he heads, published a ten-point economic plan whose first point is a deep cut in military spending, with the money moved to social services, education and healthcare.

    “A deep cut in military spending and an end to funding the war, the settlements, and the occupation.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    A programme that calls for a deep cut in military spending is a call to cut defence.

    Source: The Joint List, The socio-economic planDiscuss this
  • November 17, 2015 · StatementIslamization

    As a Knesset member he condemned the government's decision to outlaw the Northern Branch of the Islamic Movement in Israel.

    “dangerous political persecution and a serious violation of a national minority's basic right for the freedom of expression, the freedom of religion, and the freedom of assembly”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Defending an Islamic organisation against a ban is opposing action against it.

    Source: Ynetnews, Israel bans northern branch of Islamic MovementDiscuss this
  • May 25, 2026 · InterviewIslamization

    Told Channel 12 news that Hadash, Ta'al and Balad had agreed to run with the Islamic party Ra'am on the terms Ra'am asked for, a technical joint list.

    “Out of belief in the importance of the Joint List's success, we announced that we would also be ready for a technical list, as Ra'am demands”

    In Hebrew: מתוך אמונה בחשיבות הצלחת המשותפת, הכרזנו שנהיה מוכנים גם לרשימה טכנית כפי שדורשים ברע"ם

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Offering to share an election list with the Islamic party Ra'am is working with an Islamic political organisation, whoever does it.

    Source: N12 (Mako), "נוח להם שהציבור הערבי שקוע בדם": יו"ר חד"ש יוסף ג'בארין בריאיוןDiscuss this
  • September 29, 2026 · Party programmeCapitalism

    The same Joint List plan demands an end to all privatisation, the nationalisation of companies already privatised, and elsewhere rent control and a 30% tax on financial profits.

    “We will demand the cancellation of all privatisation plans, the nationalisation of companies that were privatised, democratisation in the management of government-owned companies, and the repeal of the undemocratic Economic Arrangements Law, which for years allowed cuts to welfare and the promotion of privatisations.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Cancelling privatisation and nationalising companies is the opposite of privatisation and less state ownership.

    Source: The Joint List, The socio-economic planDiscuss this
  • September 29, 2026 · Party programmeCapitalism

    The same Joint List plan, next to its tax rises on financial profits and large companies, promises no income tax up to the average wage and a cut in value added tax to 10%.

    “Alongside this, we will support an income tax exemption for employees and self-employed people earning up to the average wage in the economy, and lowering VAT to 10%.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    An income tax exemption and a lower value added tax are tax cuts, whoever proposes them.

    Source: The Joint List, The socio-economic planDiscuss this
  • March 2020 · StatementFree speech

    As a Knesset member he asked the attorney general to open a criminal investigation of Prime Minister Netanyahu and Foreign Minister Israel Katz for incitement, over their remarks calling Joint List members supporters of terror.

    “This is deranged incitement against the Arab public, and it is particularly serious when it comes from the mouth of a senior government minister against other elected officials from a weaker minority group”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Asking prosecutors to investigate politicians over what they said treats speech as a crime, whoever the target is.

    Source: Communist Party of Israel, MK Jabareen Asks AG to Probe Incitement by Netanyahu and KatzDiscuss this
  • November 2023 · StatementFree speech

    As a former Knesset member he said the arrests of Arab citizens over social media posts after 7 October 2023 had no legal basis and were meant to intimidate.

    “the continued wave of arrests being carried out by Ben-Gvir's police over the past few weeks has been targeted against Arab youth, specifically students, and constitutes a system of intimidation and suppression.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Speaking against arrests for things people wrote online is speaking against speech crimes.

    Source: Israel National News, Former MK: Ben-Gvir's police are arresting innocent ArabsDiscuss this
  • January 31, 2018 · StatementAnti-woke

    As a Knesset member he drafted a Basic Law bill that would give the Arab minority a legal right to fitting representation in every state authority, institution and decision-making body.

    “The Arab minority in the state is entitled to fitting and effective representation in all the state's governing authorities, in all its institutions, and in all its centres of decision-making”

    In Hebrew: המיעוט הערבי במדינה זכאי לייצוג הולם ואפקטיבי בכל רשויות השלטון של המדינה, בכל מוסדותיה, ובכל מוקדי קבלת ההחלטות בה

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    A law guaranteeing places in state bodies by national group is a quota by group.

    Source: Arutz Sheva (inn.co.il), פרובוקציה חדשה מבית הרשימה המשותפתDiscuss this

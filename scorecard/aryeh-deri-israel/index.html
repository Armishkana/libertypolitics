Deri, on the record

11 things Deri has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • April 2024 · StatementIran: the regime

    Days after Iran's first direct missile and drone attack on Israel in April 2024, when a strike in return was being weighed, Deri argued for restraint.

    “knowing to be restrained, to be patient, listen to our partners and not do things just for revenge and show that we did something.”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    He counselled holding back from a strike at that moment and acting with allies, which neither backs nor rejects pressure on the regime.

    Source: JFeed, Gafni joins Deri: Charedim oppose an Iran strike without complete coordination with the USDiscuss this
  • April 2026 · InterviewIran: the regime

    In an interview during the ceasefire in the 2026 war with Iran, Deri said Israel had given the Americans a plan aimed at the fall of the regime, and that he thought the ceasefire made its fall from within more likely.

    “The goal was to create conditions for the regime's fall, and I think we created those conditions.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He backed military action against the Islamic Republic and named the fall of its rulers as the aim.

    Source: Matzav, Deri: War Is Like a Cholent, Says Outcome Unpredictable as Ceasefire HoldsDiscuss this
  • September 29, 2025 · VoteMilitary

    The Shas faction, which Deri chairs, voted for the bill that added about 31 billion shekels to the 2025 state budget for defence costs, and said beforehand that it would do so because the money buys munitions and pays reservists.

    “These are truly matters of saving lives, and therefore the Shas faction will vote for the proposal”

    In Hebrew: אלו עניינים של פיקוח נפש ממש, ומשכך, סיעת ש״ס תצביע בעד ההצעה

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    A vote for extra money for the armed forces in wartime is a vote to fund and equip them.

    Source: Kikar HaShabbat, Shas supported, Degel opposed, Eichler and Porush abstained: the defence budget was approved in the plenumDiscuss this
  • June 7, 2026 · StatementMilitary

    Deri visited the military prison where yeshiva students were held for not obeying call-up orders, met about ten of them and called for the arrests to stop.

    “From here we cry out: stop treating Torah students as criminals”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    He opposes enforcing conscription on men the army has called up.

    Source: Israel National News, Haredi MKs spark firestorm with visit to draft dodgersDiscuss this
  • March 15, 2018 · StatementBorders

    As interior minister, after the Supreme Court temporarily barred the deportation of thousands of people who had entered Israel illegally from Africa, Deri said the deportations would go on.

    “We will respond to the Supreme Court with our response to these frivolous appeals, and then the deportation of the infiltrators - which I started - will continue.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He ran and defended a programme to deport people who entered the country illegally.

    Source: Israel National News, We'll deport the infiltrators - justice will prevail in the endDiscuss this
  • January 14, 2016 · DecreeIslamization

    As interior minister, Deri signed orders barring Sheikh Raed Salah, head of the outlawed northern branch of the Islamic Movement, his deputy and other activists from leaving the country.

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He used his office against the leaders of a banned organisation tied to the Muslim Brotherhood.

    Source: Israel National News, Raed Salah banned from leaving IsraelDiscuss this
  • March 21, 2021 · InterviewIslamization

    Asked before the March 2021 election whether he would sit in a coalition supported by the Islamic party Ra'am, Deri did not rule it out; he said he would rather rely on his own bloc's 61 seats and that Mansour Abbas could give his support later if he wished.

    “I cannot say no or yes because I did not get into the thick of things”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    He neither accepted nor refused governing with the support of the party of the southern Islamic Movement.

    Source: Israel National News, Deri does not rule out coalition with Mansour Abbas, but 'would rather rely on our 61'Discuss this
  • July 13, 2026 · StatementSecularism

    When the Knesset passed the Basic Law: Torah Study by 63 votes to 52, a law the Haredi parties had demanded from the prime minister before the Knesset dissolved, Deri welcomed it.

    “For the first time, the Jewish state recognizes the supreme value of the holy Torah and the status of those who study it.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    The law writes a religious value and the standing of those who study religion full time into the country's constitutional laws.

    Source: Israel National News, Watch: Knesset passes Basic Law of Torah StudyDiscuss this
  • March 2023 · StatementCapitalism

    When the finance minister scrapped the tax on sugary drinks brought in by the previous government, a step the Haredi parties had demanded, Deri applauded the decision and called the tax a political measure aimed at underprivileged people.

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    He backed removing a tax on consumer goods.

    Source: Matzav, Israel Scraps Tax On Sugary Drinks, Sparking Health ConcernsDiscuss this
  • December 3, 2025 · StatementCapitalism

    In a dispute with the finance ministry over who qualifies for state food vouchers, Deri's office said he would keep fighting to restore the earlier rules, under which about 400,000 families received vouchers.

    “Rav Deri will not stay silent and will continue the battle until the criteria are restored to full equality.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Food vouchers paid from the state budget to hundreds of thousands of households are more state spending.

    Source: Matzav, Shas Slams Channel 12: We Will Not Stay Silent While Poor Chareidim Are Pushed AsideDiscuss this
  • February 15, 2026 · StatementFree speech

    When former prime minister Naftali Bennett came to Bnei Brak to speak against an attack on female soldiers there, Deri called him an inciter and asked the police to bar him from the city.

    “I call on the police commissioner to immediately issue a restraining order barring Naftali Bennett from the streets of Bnei Brak.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    He asked the state to keep a political rival who had come to speak out of a city's public streets.

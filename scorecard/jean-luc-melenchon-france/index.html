Mélenchon, on the record

14 things Mélenchon has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • December 6, 2025 · StatementTyranny

    Told the parliamentary inquiry into links between parties and Islamic radical networks that his movement would never accept religious infiltration.

    “Our movement will never accept religious infiltration”

    In French: Notre mouvement n'acceptera jamais l'entrisme religieux

    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    It is a statement of principle with no act for or against any organisation, so it does not settle the measure.

    Source: LCP, Jean-Luc Mélenchon : "Notre mouvement n'acceptera jamais l'entrisme religieux"Discuss this
  • May 8, 2026 · InterviewTyranny

    Said that as president he would not follow the American and Israeli war on Iran and would try to make those who launched it back down.

    “I would try to make those who decided on their own, and in violation of international law, to intervene in a country, back down”

    In French: J'essaierais de faire en sorte que ceux qui ont décidé tout seul, et en violation du droit international, une intervention sur un pays, de les faire reculer

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Condemning the strikes on the regime and working to stop them counts as against liberty.

    “I do not believe the world would be more stable and better company with more countries that have nuclear weapons”

    In French: Je ne crois pas que le monde serait plus stable et mieux fréquentable avec plus de pays qui ont les armes nucléaires

    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

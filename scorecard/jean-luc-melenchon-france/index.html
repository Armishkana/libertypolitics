Mélenchon, on the record

14 things Mélenchon has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes in Congress. A line that does not settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • May 8, 2026 · InterviewIran: the regime

    Said that as president he would not follow the American and Israeli war on Iran and would try to make those who launched it back down.

    “I would try to make those who decided on their own, and in violation of international law, to intervene in a country, back down”

    In French: J'essaierais de faire en sorte que ceux qui ont décidé tout seul, et en violation du droit international, une intervention sur un pays, de les faire reculer

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    Condemning the strikes on the regime and working to stop them counts as against liberty.

  • May 8, 2026 · InterviewIran: the regime

    In the same interview said he thinks very badly of the Iranian regime and that Iran must be kept from getting a nuclear weapon.

    “I do not believe the world would be more stable and better company with more countries that have nuclear weapons”

    In French: Je ne crois pas que le monde serait plus stable et mieux fréquentable avec plus de pays qui ont les armes nucléaires

    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    He is against an Iranian bomb but names no pressure he would apply, so the line does not settle the measure.

    Source: TF1 Info, Moyen-Orient, Algérie, Chine... Ce qu'il faut retenir de l'interview de Jean-Luc Mélenchon sur LCIDiscuss this

    When President Macron announced France would recognise a Palestinian state in September 2025, asked why not at once and called for an arms embargo.

    “Why in September and not now? And the arms embargo?”

    In French: Pourquoi en septembre et pas maintenant ? Et l'embargo sur les armes ?

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    An arms embargo on Israel and immediate recognition of a Palestinian state while Hamas held hostages counts as against liberty.

    Against liberty

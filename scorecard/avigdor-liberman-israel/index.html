Liberman, on the record

16 things Liberman has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes in Congress. A line that doesn't settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • May 10, 2018 · Military operationTyranny

    As defence minister he said Israel had struck almost all of the Islamic Republic's military infrastructure in Syria after the regime's forces fired rockets at Israel.

    “We hit ... almost all of the Iranian infrastructure in Syria”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Striking a regime's forces after they attack is deterring a regime that rules by fear.

    Source: Algemeiner, Israeli Defense Minister: We Hit Almost All Iranian Infrastructure in SyriaDiscuss this
  • October 24, 2018 · Government actTyranny

    As defence minister he decided to resume deliveries of Qatari-bought fuel into the Gaza Strip, citing fewer violent incidents on the border.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Easing supplies paid for by Qatar into a territory a terror group rules, after weeks of attacks, is lifting pressure on it.

    Source: TPS, Liberman renews Qatari fuel delivery to Gaza as border violence wanesDiscuss this
  • November 14, 2018 · Government actTyranny

    As defence minister he resigned in protest at the government's ceasefire with Hamas after about 500 rockets were fired at southern Israel, and called for early elections.

    “500 rockets were fired on Israel's south and the response was not sufficient and not appropriate. We're buying short-term quiet, with the price being severe long-term damage to national security.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Refusing to stay in a government that buys quiet from a terror group is refusing a deal that keeps it alive.

    Source: Globes, Defense Minister Avigdor Liberman resignsDiscuss this
  • June 16, 2026 · InterviewTyranny

    In a radio interview in which he opposed the agreement between the United States and the Islamic Republic, he said that as prime minister he would order the Mossad to work only on bringing down the regime in Iran.

    “If I were prime minister, I would summon the Mossad director and tell him: 'Don't deal with anything else anymore, except toppling the Iranian regime,'”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Opposing a deal with the Islamic Republic and calling for action to remove the regime is pressure on the Islamic Republic.

    Source: The Jerusalem Post, Liberman: Netanyahu's submission, US-Iran deal will allow Tehran to evolve into a nuclear powerDiscuss this
  • September 23, 2026 · StatementTyranny

    His party announced it would vote to disqualify the Islamic party Ra'am, along with the Joint List and two candidates, from running for the Knesset, saying any party that opposes eliminating Hamas has no place there.

    “Therefore, we will vote in favor of disqualifying the Joint List, Ra'am, Ofer Cassif, and Sami Abu Shehadeh.”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Ra'am is a legal party in Israel's parliament. Governing with it, or trying to bar it from the ballot, is judged by what the government then does, not by the religion of a partner, so this line does not settle the test.

    Source: Israel National News, Yisrael Beytenu: We'll vote to disqualify Ra'amDiscuss this
  • June 26, 2018 · PostFree nations

    As defence minister, during street protests in Iran, he published a post in Persian addressed to Iranians, saying their rulers were spending the country's money on armed groups abroad while they struggled to buy bread.

    “Even last month, when you were fighting for your bread in the streets of Tehran, Qassem Soleimani took several actions, including a logistical operation intended for Syria at the price of $70 million.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Addressing Iranian protesters and taking their side against their rulers is speaking for Iranians.

    Source: The Algemeiner, Israeli Defense Minister Encourages Iranian Protesters, Asks, 'Where Is All Your Money Going?'Discuss this
  • September 2022 · InterviewFree nations

    As finance minister, asked in an interview about his position on the war in Ukraine, he did not condemn Russia and said Israel was taking a balanced line between the two countries.

    “I am not for Russia, I am not for Ukraine, I am for Israel”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Declaring neutrality between Kyiv and Moscow is the opposite of standing with Ukraine against Putin.

    Source: The Jerusalem Post, Ukrainian ambassador bashes Liberman for not denouncing RussiaDiscuss this
  • October 11, 2023 · StatementFree nations

    From the opposition after the 7 October 2023 attack, he pledged his party's full backing to the government for any action leading to the elimination of Hamas, asking nothing in return.

    “I welcome the announcement of the Prime Minister and the Minister of Defense, we will support any action that will lead to the elimination of Hamas.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Backing the country's defense against the terror army that attacked it is standing with a democracy under attack.

    Source: Israel National News, Liberman: This government has 100% backing from Yisrael BeytenuDiscuss this
  • June 16, 2026 · InterviewFree nations

    In the radio interview in which he opposed the agreement between the United States and the Islamic Republic, he thanked the Americans and said he had no complaints against them, while saying the two countries' interests differ and that Israel can tell the American president no.

    “I have no complaints against the Americans. There are people here who expect the US to act according to Israeli interests, but no. I thank the US for what they have done”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    He thanks the ally and in the same breath urges standing against it on this point, so the words do not settle the measure.

    Source: The Jerusalem Post, Avigdor Liberman: Israel must tell Donald Trump 'no' on deal or Iran will develop nuclear powerDiscuss this
  • May 25, 2022 · StatementFree speech

    As finance minister he ordered a review aimed at cutting the state budget of Ben-Gurion University after it allowed a student demonstration at which flags of the Palestine Liberation Organization were flown.

    “Therefore, I instructed that the management of the university be examined for the purpose of activating my authority to reduce its funding.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Using a minister's power over funding to punish a university for what was said at a lawful protest is government pressure on speech.

    Source: Israel National News, Finance Minister weighs budget cuts to Negev universityDiscuss this
  • October 12, 2021 · SpeechFree markets

    As finance minister he said his budget would raise certain taxes, naming a higher purchase tax to cool the housing market and a doubling of the tax on disposable plasticware.

    “Certain taxes will be raised to serve different economic goals.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Raising taxes to steer what people buy is higher taxation and more state direction of the market.

    Source: Israel National News, Liberman: Certain taxes will be raised for different economic goalsDiscuss this
  • February 9, 2022 · StatementFree markets

    As finance minister he presented a plan that cut income tax for working parents and cut import duties on basic foods, furniture and kitchen equipment. The same plan also had the state pay a recent electricity price rise for low-paid workers and subsidise after-school care.

    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Lower income tax and lower import duties are tax cuts and freer trade.

    Source: Globes, Israeli gov't unveils plan to cut cost of livingDiscuss this
  • July 7, 2021 · DecreeConscience

    As finance minister he changed the rules for state daycare subsidies so that families in which the father studies full time in a religious seminary and does not work no longer qualified.

    “The decision I am presenting today is a very important step, and it changes the priorities in this country, placing first and foremost those who work and pay taxes.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Ending a state subsidy that went to full-time religious students is ending a religious exemption paid for by the state.

    Source: Israel National News, Finance Minister to cut daycare subsidies for yeshiva familiesDiscuss this
  • May 30, 2022 · StatementConscience

    As finance minister in a coalition that included the Islamic party Ra'am, he agreed with that party to fund 35 new state posts for Muslim imams, and his office said the money came from agreements made with Ra'am.

    “The funds were anchored in agreements with the Ra'am party several months ago.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    State money for religious posts agreed with a party in return for its support is a state favour to religious bodies, against liberty on conscience.

    Source: Israel National News, With Shaked's support: Liberman's agreement with Ra'amDiscuss this
  • December 7, 2025 · Party programmeConscience

    His party drew up five principles it will insist on in any government, among them public transport and open businesses on the Sabbath by local decision, civil marriage and divorce, and repeal of the law that widened the powers of rabbinical courts.

    “Tel Aviv will not impose Shabbat transportation on Bnei Brak, and Bnei Brak will not impose its way of life on Tel Aviv”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Civil marriage and ending religious control over transport and trade on the Sabbath take religious law out of public life.

    Source: Ynetnews, Avigdor Liberman's Yisrael Beiteinu draws up 5-point pledge ahead of next governmentDiscuss this
  • August 17, 2026 · PostDefense

    He set as a condition for joining any coalition a draft law that requires every Israeli citizen to do military or civilian service, and rejected a partial exemption for young ultra-Orthodox men.

    “There will be no exemption for 30%, 3%, or even a single person”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Demanding that everyone be drafted backs the conscription the country's army depends on.

    Source: Israel National News, Liberman lays out coalition red lines ahead of Israeli electionsDiscuss this

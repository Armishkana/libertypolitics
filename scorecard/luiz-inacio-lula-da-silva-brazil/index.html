    Under his government the Navy let two Islamic Republic warships dock in Rio de Janeiro after the United States asked Brazil not to. He had first put the visit off until after his meeting with President Biden.

    FAgainst liberty

    Opening the BRICS summit in Rio, after the armed conflict between the Islamic Republic, Israel and the United States, he said his government had denounced the violations of Iran's territorial integrity.

    “The Brazilian government denounced the violations of Iran's territorial integrity, as it had already done in the case of Ukraine.”

    In Portuguese: O governo brasileiro denunciou as violações à integridade territorial do Irã, como já havia feito no caso da Ucrânia.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Denouncing military action against the regime as a violation of its territory counts as against liberty.

    By the test I use for votes

    “What is happening in the Gaza Strip to the Palestinian people has not existed at any other moment in history. In fact, it has: when Hitler decided to kill the Jews”

    In Portuguese: O que está acontecendo na Faixa de Gaza com o povo palestino, não existe em nenhum outro momento histórico. Aliás, existiu quando Hitler resolveu matar os judeus

    FAgainst liberty

    Likening Israel's war to the Holocaust goes further than calling it genocide, which counts as against liberty.

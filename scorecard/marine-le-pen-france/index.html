Le Pen, on the record

14 things Le Pen has said and done. Each one has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes in Congress. A line that does not settle the question is marked as not clear and counts for nothing.

  • June 19, 2025 · StatementIran: the regime

    Said at the Paris Air Show, during the June 2025 war between Israel and Iran, that the disappearance of the mullahs' regime would be a good thing.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Wanting the Islamic Republic gone while it was under military attack is on the side of pressure on the regime.

    Source: BFMTV, Conflit Israël-Iran: "La disparition du régime des mollahs (...) serait une bonne chose", réagit Marine Le Pen (RN) au salon du BourgetDiscuss this
  • March 25, 2026 · InterviewIran: the regime

    Said the United States made a mistake in attacking Iran because it expected the regime to fall within days, and called the regime extremely solid.

    “What I see is that the United States has clearly made a mistake, in that they thought the Iranian regime would fall within a few days. It has not fallen. The Iranian regime is an extremely solid regime”

    In French: Ce que je vois, c'est que les Etats-Unis ont manifestement commis une erreur en ce sens qu'ils pensaient que, en l'espace de quelques jours, le régime iranien tomberait. Il n'est pas tombé. Le régime iranien est un régime extrêmement solide

    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    She criticises how the war on the regime was planned without saying whether she backs or opposes the strikes, so the line does not settle the measure.

    Source: Brut (AFP), Iran : pour Le Pen, les États-Unis "ont commis une erreur" car le régime "n'est pas tombé"Discuss this
  • September 22, 2025 · PostIsrael

    Opposed France's recognition of a Palestinian state in September 2025, saying it recognised a Hamas state.

    “By making this recognition today, with no condition at all, it is Hamastan and not Palestine that Emmanuel Macron is recognising”

    In French: En effectuant cette reconnaissance aujourd'hui, sans aucune condition, c'est le Hamastan et non la Palestine que reconnaît Emmanuel Macron

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Opposing recognition of a Palestinian state while Hamas held hostages is the side of treating Israel as an ally.

    Source: LCP, La France va reconnaître l'État de Palestine ce lundi, une décision contestée par certainsDiscuss this
  • April 2022 · Party programmeAllies

    Promised in the 2022 presidential campaign to take France out of NATO's integrated command.

    “leave NATO's integrated command”

    In French: quitter le commandement intégré de l'Otan

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    Wanting out of NATO's command is against liberty by our test.

    Source: TF1 Info (AFP), Otan : pourquoi le RN a changé d'avis sur la sortie de la France du commandement intégréDiscuss this
  • March 12, 2024 · VoteAllies

    Her group abstained when the National Assembly voted on the security agreement with Ukraine in March 2024; she said the government was using the crisis for electoral ends.

    “Either you are pro-Macron, or you are accused of being pro-Putin”

    In French: Soit on est pro-Macron, soit on est accusé d'être pro-Poutine

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    Withholding a vote for the Ukraine security agreement is not standing with Ukraine against Russia.

    Source: LCP, L'Assemblée nationale soutient largement l'accord avec l'Ukraine, sans le RN qui s'abstient et LFI qui vote contreDiscuss this
  • September 16, 2026 · InterviewAllies

    Said France can keep training Ukraine's soldiers and sending equipment but can no longer give Ukraine money, and that President Macron was right to refuse to send French jets to protect Kyiv's sky.

    “We can go on training their soldiers, their army, go on bringing them equipment to defend themselves, but financially we no longer can”

    In French: On peut continuer à former leurs soldats, leur armée, continuer à leur apporter du matériel pour se défendre, mais financièrement nous ne pouvons plus

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    Ending financial aid cuts support for Ukraine, even though she would keep training and equipment.

    Source: TF1 Info, Marine Le Pen dans "Objectif 2027" sur LCI : les temps forts de l'émissionDiscuss this
  • December 10, 2025 · SpeechMilitary

    Said the rise in the defence budget since 2017 is one of the few policies to President Macron's credit, in a debate where the Assembly backed 6.7 billion euros more for defence in 2026.

    “It is in fact one of the few policies that can be put to the credit of the President of the Republic”

    In French: C'est d'ailleurs l'une des rares politiques que l'on peut mettre à l'actif du président de la République

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Approving a rising defence budget is the side of funding the armed forces.

    Source: LCP, Vote sur la défense nationale à l'Assemblée : ce qu'il faut retenir du débat en 6 pointsDiscuss this
  • 2022 · Party programmeBorders

    Her presidential programme, still published by her party, promises to deport illegal immigrants and foreign offenders systematically.

    “Systematically deport illegal immigrants and foreign offenders and criminals.”

    In French: Expulser systématiquement les clandestins, délinquants et criminels étrangers.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Deporting those who break immigration law counts as with liberty.

    Source: Rassemblement National, 22 mesures pour 2022Discuss this
  • October 30, 2025 · VoteIslamic migration

    The group she leads tabled the resolution to end the 1968 agreement that gives Algerians special rights to settle in France; the Assembly passed it by one vote and she called the day historic.

    “It is a day that can be called historic for the RN”

    In French: C'est une journée qu'on peut qualifier d'historique pour le RN

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Ending a special entry and residence route from Algeria limits migration from an Islamic country.

    Source: LCP, Dénonciation des accords franco-algériens de 1968 : un texte du RN adopté, à une voix près, à l'AssembléeDiscuss this

    Her programme promises to root out the ideologies behind Islamization and their networks in France, with a bill written for that purpose.

    “Eradicate Islamist ideologies and all their networks from the national territory.”

    In French: Éradiquer les idéologies islamistes et l'ensemble de leurs réseaux du territoire national.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Acting against organisations that push religious rule counts as with liberty.

    Source: Rassemblement National, 22 mesures pour 2022Discuss this
  • August 31, 2026 · StatementIslamization

    Confirmed that if elected she would put to a referendum a ban, with penalties, on the Islamic veil in public space.

    “the penalisation of the Islamist veil in public space”

    In French: la pénalisation du voile islamiste dans l'espace public

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    A ban on the veil is counted by this measure as acting against Islamization; hers goes further than schools and public office.

    Source: BFMTV, Présidentielle 2027: Marine Le Pen confirme vouloir soumettre au référendum "la pénalisation du voile islamiste dans l'espace public"Discuss this
  • August 27, 2026 · SpeechCapitalism

    Told the employers' federation she would bring the legal retirement age back down to 62, and to 60 for people who started work before 20.

    “then gradually we will get to 42 years of contributions and a legal age of 62”

    In French: puis progressivement on arrivera à 42 ans de cotisations et un âge légal de 62 ans

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    Undoing the pension reform and lowering the retirement age is more state spending, the side against liberty.

    Source: LCP, Présidentielle 2027: ce qu'il faut retenir du premier débat entre les candidatsDiscuss this
  • August 27, 2026 · SpeechCapitalism

    Announced a plan to cut public spending by 125 billion euros.

    “We will present our path to restoring the public finances, with 125 billion euros of savings”

    In French: Nous présenterons notre trajectoire de rétablissement des finances publiques avec 125 milliards d'euros d'économies

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    Cutting state spending counts as with liberty.

    Source: LCP, Présidentielle 2027: ce qu'il faut retenir du premier débat entre les candidatsDiscuss this

    With liberty

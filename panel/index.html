Get on the panel

Liberty Politics is a live panel show on YouTube, on most days. There's room on the panel, and you can apply for a seat.

It covers U.S. politics, Israel and Iran, the rest of the Middle East, and world affairs like Ukraine, Europe and China.

Apply with a short video

About ten minutes. The seat is unpaid.

What you get

  • A seat on a live show, in front of a live audience.
  • Your own channel plugged on air.
  • As much or as little as suits you: one show now and then, or several a week.

What it asks of you

  • A camera, a decent microphone and a stable connection.
  • Your camera on for the whole show, with your own face.
  • That you show up, follow the show and let other people speak. It's a discussion.
  • Any politics. Communists, Muslims, Democrats, all welcome. The panel isn't meant to be an echo chamber.
  • A short video first: one to three minutes of you talking to camera. The form tells you what to say and how to send it.

What happens after you apply

  1. Armin Navabi, who hosts the show, watches every video.
  2. The people who fit get an email back.
  3. The video comes first for everyone, so please don't ask for a call before it. Sending one doesn't guarantee a seat.

Candidate lists: the state election offices, as compiled on Wikipedia, checked against the Federal Election Commission.

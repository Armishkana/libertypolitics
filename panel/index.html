Join the Liberty Politics Team

The team is the people who come on Liberty Politics, a live show on YouTube, and talk politics in front of a live audience. Joining is free.

The show covers U.S. politics, Israel and Iran, the rest of the Middle East, and world affairs like Ukraine, Europe and China.

Apply to join

About five minutes. Nobody pays to join, and the seats are unpaid.

What you get

  • A seat on the live panels. Come on when it suits you: one show now and then, or several a week.
  • Your channel in front of a live audience. Your own channel gets plugged on air.
  • The Team mark beside your name on this site. Everything you post here carries it, so readers can see you're on the team.
  • The rest of the team. You meet them on the panels, and talk with them in the team's own room on this site.
  • A brand kit for your channel. Coming soon.

What it asks of you

  • A camera, a decent microphone and a stable connection.
  • Your camera on for the whole show, with your own face.
  • That you show up, follow the show and let other people speak. It's a discussion.
  • Any politics. Communists, Muslims, Democrats, all welcome. The team isn't meant to be an echo chamber.

Apply to join

When you press Send, you make a free account first (or sign in). The Team mark goes on that account.

YouTube, X, TikTok, Substack, anywhere people find you. Leave it empty if you don't have one yet.

What do you make?

Filled in from your device. Change it if it's wrong.

Sent. Thank you.

Armin Navabi, who hosts the show, reads every application. The people who fit get an email back.

What happens after you apply

  1. Armin Navabi, who hosts the show, reads every application and watches your channel.
  2. The people who fit get an email back.
  3. Sending one doesn't guarantee a seat.

What you send is kept with your account. Only you and the people who run this site can read it. Delete your account and it goes too.

What would you bring to the team?

Say it where other readers can answer you. Opinions, videos and corrections about the team are all in one place.

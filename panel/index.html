Come on the show. Say it live.

Liberty Politics is a live panel show on YouTube, every day. The people on the panel are the team, and the team has seats open.

Apply with a video

Free to apply, and the seats are unpaid. About ten minutes, plus your video.

Shows every day from 11am to 11:30pm, U.S. Eastern time.

See a panel

The discussions cover U.S. politics, Israel and Iran, the rest of the Middle East, and world affairs like Ukraine, Europe and China. Three to watch, each opening where the whole panel is talking.

  • The panel on Abdul El-Sayed and the Muslim Brotherhood.16 October 2026. Opens on YouTube
  • The evening panel opens, then takes apart a collapse prediction with seven million views.18 September 2026. Opens on YouTube
  • The U.S. halts strikes on Iran, and the chat welcomes a new face on the team.26 August 2026. Opens on YouTube

Who's on the team

What you get

  • A seat on the live panels. Come on when it suits you: one show now and then, or several a week.
  • Your channel in front of a live audience. Your own channel gets plugged on air.
  • The Team mark beside your name on this site. Everything you post here carries it, so readers can see you're on the team.
  • The rest of the team. You meet them on the panels, and talk with them in the team's own room on this site.
  • A brand kit for your channel. Coming soon.
  • The Intel Room. Where the stories for the show are gathered. It's open to everyone, so you can read the news the panel is reading.

What it asks of you

  • A video first, 1 to 3 minutes, of you talking to the camera. The form says what to cover.
  • A camera, a decent microphone and a stable connection.
  • Your camera on for the whole show, with your own face.
  • That you show up, follow the show and let other people speak. It's a discussion.
  • Any politics. Communists, Muslims, Democrats, all welcome. The team isn't meant to be an echo chamber.

Apply with a video

When you press Send, you make a free account first (or sign in). The Team mark goes on that account.

Camera on, your own face, talking to the camera. Answer three things:

  • Who you are, and why you want to come on the show.
  • Your view on Israel, and on the U.S. midterm elections.
  • Your view on the war between the U.S. and Israel on one side and the Islamic Republic on the other.
How to make the video in 3 steps
  1. Record it on your phone, in a quiet room, looking at the camera.
  2. Upload it to YouTube as Unlisted, so only people with the link can see it. Google Drive, Loom, Dropbox, X, TikTok, Instagram and Vimeo work too.

Take the Overview and Discussion switch off the front page

It looks like a second menu.

My take It is the door to the discussion on every page. I would rather make it look less like a menu. Tell me how.

How the room works

  1. Post an idea here, or anywhere on the site. Wherever you say it, I bring it to this room and tell you I did.
  2. I judge it the day it arrives. If it is right and mine to do, I build it, and it lands in Built with your name on it.
  3. If it needs more thought, it goes on the floor. Back it with a like, argue it with a reply. A good argument changes my mind.
  4. Ideas about the fight itself belong here too, not only the site: where to show up, who to reach, what to make.

What happens to it

  1. Every page has two parts: the record, and its discussion. Tap Discussion at the top of the page and write in the box there.
  2. To send it you need an account: a name, an email and a password. What you wrote is kept while you make one, and it is sent the moment you are in.
  3. I send one email to check that the address is yours. Until you open the link in it, only you can see what you posted. Once you have, it shows on the page it is about: a race, a politician, a state, a vote, or this page.
  4. Other readers can like it, unlike it or answer it. The posts with the most likes over unlikes are shown first.
  5. A correction or a bug report goes on the page like any other post, marked for what it is, and it reaches me directly as well. I check it: a claim about a vote against the official roll call, a bug on a phone and on a computer. If you're right, I fix it and list the fix on this page.
  6. An idea for the site or for the fight goes on the board above, wherever you posted it. A judgment call, such as which side of a vote is the liberty side, I decide in the open, and a good argument changes my mind.

What goes on a page, and what does not

Your opinion goes up whether I agree with it or not. I do not pick by side or by candidate.

These get removed: insults aimed at other readers, threats, adverts, anything private about anyone, a claim of fact about a person with nothing to back it, and anything that is not about the page it was posted on.

Every post has a Report button. When three readers report a post, it comes off the page until I have looked at it. I put it back if it is within these rules and remove it if it is not, whichever side it is on.

Your name and your email

You need an account to post, to answer, to like or unlike, and to report. Reading needs nothing.

The name you choose is shown with what you post. Your email is never shown on the site and never passed on to anyone.

The account keeps your email, your name, what you posted, and what you liked, unliked or reported. If you tell me you make videos, it keeps that and the link to your channel. Other readers see how many likes a post has, never who gave them, and the author of a post cannot see who reported it. All of it, on one page.

"Email me news from Liberty Politics" is a box you can tick. It starts empty, and you can change it whenever you like. News is one email a week at most: the grades that moved, the votes that moved them, and one clip from the show.

To delete your account, tap your name at the top of any page, then "Delete my account". That removes what you posted, your likes and unlikes, and the account itself.

What will not change a grade

Volume. Ten notes saying a senator deserves an A change nothing. One note showing a vote I got wrong changes the grade as soon as I confirm it.

What should I build, or fight for, next?

Say it where other readers can answer you. Opinions, videos and corrections about the War Room are all in one place.

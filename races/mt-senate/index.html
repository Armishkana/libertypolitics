Kurt Alme, on the record

  • March 2026 · StatementIran: the regime

    Asked about the War Powers Resolution, he said it is dangerous to tie the commander in chief's hands while Iran seeks nuclear weapons.

    “It's dangerous to tie our commander-in-chief and troops' hands behind their backs. Especially when Americans are being targeted by adversaries and threats, and the world's largest state sponsor of terror is trying to get its hands on nuclear weapons.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Opposing war powers limits that would stop action against Iran is the with side; he also said Congress must enforce its role, so this is not a blanket rejection of oversight.

  • October 5, 2026 · StatementIran: the regime

    Asked how to lower living costs, she listed ending the war with Iran as one of her three actions.

    “end the war with Iran that is pushing fuel prices higher.”
    By the test I use for votes
    ?Not clear either way

    Calling for the war to end, with no policy, bill or vote named, does not settle the Iran regime measure.

    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Cutting off weapons to Israel and calling its war genocide are the against side of this measure.

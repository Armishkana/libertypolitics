  • February 28, 2026 · His campaign siteIran: the regime

    On the day the United States began airstrikes on Iran, backed the operation and called Iran an imminent threat.

    “Iran is an imminent threat to our country and must not have a nuclear weapon.”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    The measure is maximum pressure on the regime in Tehran. We count a vote to stop American action against it as against liberty; he backed the action.

Abdul El-Sayed, on the record

El-Sayed has no votes in Congress since 2021, so there is no letter grade. This is what El-Sayed has said and done on our measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes.

  • May 2026 · His campaign siteIran: the regime

    Opposes the United States war in Iran and wants a deal on the model of the 2015 nuclear agreement instead.

    “While I oppose a nuclear-armed Iran, I understand that diplomatic means of achieving that goal are more effective and come with a far lower human cost, just as President Obama had achieved under the JCPOA.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count a vote to stop American action against the regime as against liberty, and a deal that leaves the regime in place the same way.

    Source: Abdul for Senate, Priorities: Money Out of PoliticsReport this entry
  • July 19, 2026 · InterviewIran: the regime

    Asked on CNN why he told staff he did not want to comment on the killing of Iran's Supreme Leader, Ali Khamenei, said the war should never have been fought.

    “So, yes, I didn't want to comment on it then because it's a ridiculous pretext.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    The measure is maximum pressure on the regime in Tehran. Opposing the war that removed its leader is the other side of that.

    Source: CNN, State of the Union transcriptReport this entry
  • September 23, 2026 · News reportIran: the people

    Asked at a University of Michigan town hall to stand against the Revolutionary Guard and with Iranian protesters, said he stands with movements for freedom and democracy, and that who leads Iran is for Iranians to decide.

    “One can stand in solidarity with freedom and democracy movements, but my worry is when one decides that we are going to go in and take out the leadership of a foreign government”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    He says he stands with freedom movements, and he did not say he stands against the Revolutionary Guard. That does not settle it either way, so it counts for nothing.

    Source: Jewish Insider, El-Sayed avoids acknowledging Iranian protester deathsReport this entry
  • May 2026 · His campaign siteIsrael

    Calls for an immediate American arms embargo on Israel, including defensive weapons.

    “I support an immediate arms embargo on Israel and reject the false differentiation between “offensive” and “defensive” weapons.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count a vote to block a single weapons sale to Israel as against liberty. An embargo blocks all of them.

    Source: Abdul for Senate, Priorities: Money Out of PoliticsReport this entry
  • July 19, 2026 · InterviewIsrael

    Said on CNN that he questions whether Israel has a right to American tax dollars.

    “Whatever you think about the right of Israel to exist, I wonder if they have a right to our tax dollars.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count votes for security aid to Israel as with liberty, and votes to cut it as against.

    Source: CNN, State of the Union transcriptReport this entry
  • August 9, 2026 · InterviewAllies

    Said on NBC's Meet the Press that he supports American military aid to Ukraine.

    “So when we’re talking about taking on Putin and his violation of international law, I do believe we have a responsibility to uphold international law and, therefore, to support Ukraine in its efforts.”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    We count a vote for aid to Ukraine as with liberty.

    Source: NBC News, Meet the Press transcriptReport this entry
  • October 3, 2026 · Candidate questionnaireMilitary

    Wrote in a Detroit Free Press candidate questionnaire that the military budget should be cut.

    “We need to curb our over-inflated military budget that is being spent to make war that only makes us less safe and raises our energy prices.”

    Against liberty

    The measure is funding and equipping the armed forces. We count a vote for the yearly defense bill as with liberty.

    Against liberty

    Against liberty

    Asked whether she supports military action in Iran, she says she strongly opposes the Iran war.

    “I strongly oppose the Iran War. It is an unconstitutional war of choice with no clear objectives and, now, no simple exit strategy.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Opposing American strikes and war on the Iranian regime is the against side of this measure.

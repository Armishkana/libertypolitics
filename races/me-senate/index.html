Troy Jackson, on the record

Jackson has no votes in Congress since 2021, so the grade above comes from this list: what Jackson has said and done on our measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes.

  • September 2, 2026 · News reportIran: the regime

    Called the war in Iran ridiculous and said Senator Collins, as chair of the Appropriations Committee, should cut off the money for it.

    “Well, yeah, there’s something you can do. I mean, you can shut off the money. And that is what she should do as chair of appropriations. If she doesn’t agree with this Iran war”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count a vote to stop American action against the regime in Tehran as against liberty. Cutting off the money for the war does the same thing.

    Source: WABI, Collins, Jackson response on Iran war authorizationDiscuss this
  • September 30, 2026 · News reportIsrael

    Apologized for saying it was unfortunate that some people in Maine reject the word genocide for the war in Gaza only because they are Jewish, kept the word, and said he would fight antisemitism in the Senate.

    “In the U.S. Senate, I’ll fight antisemitism and work for lasting peace and security for Palestinians and Israelis.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    A pledge to fight antisemitism and work for peace does not say whether he treats Israel as an ally. It settles nothing, so it counts for nothing.

    Source: Jewish Insider, Troy Jackson: 'Unfortunate' that Jewish Mainers reject 'genocide' label for Gaza warDiscuss this
  • October 2026 · His campaign siteIsrael

    Lists among his priorities an end to American tax dollars for Israel's military, and calls the war in Gaza a genocide.

    “End the genocide in Gaza. No U.S. tax dollars to Israel's military.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count votes for security aid to Israel as with liberty, and votes to cut it as against.

    Source: Troy Jackson for Maine, Troy's priorities in the SenateDiscuss this
  • July 16, 2026 · News reportAllies

    At the first debate among the Democrats seeking the nomination, supported American aid to Ukraine, as every candidate on the stage did, according to Reuters.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    We count a vote for aid to Ukraine as with liberty.

    Source: Reuters, via Yahoo News: Maine Democratic Senate candidates brand Collins a Trump enabler in debateDiscuss this
  • August 5, 2026 · Campaign statement in a news reportMilitary

    His campaign told the Bangor Daily News that he would put diplomacy ahead of open-ended military intervention and would not have Washington enrich defense contractors.

    “Troy would demand real congressional oversight, put diplomacy ahead of open-ended military intervention, rebuild relationships with allies like Canada and make sure Washington is investing in American workers and communities — not enriching defense contractors or multinational corporations”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    The measure is funding and equipping the armed forces. This does not say whether he would vote for the yearly defense bill, so it counts for nothing.

    Source: Bangor Daily News, How Troy Jackson differs from Susan Collins on foreign policyDiscuss this
  • September 21, 2026 · News reportBorders

    In written answers to the Portland Press Herald, said the border must be secured and immigration enforcement handed to a different government agency.

    “We need to secure the border, and we need to return immigration enforcement to a professional government agency that doesn’t target Black and brown people at the whim of Donald Trump”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    A secure border is the measure. Taken with the line above, his record on it is mixed.

    Source: Portland Press Herald, Where Susan Collins and Troy Jackson stand on immigration policyDiscuss this
  • October 2026 · His campaign siteBorders

    Lists among his priorities abolishing Immigration and Customs Enforcement, the agency that carries out deportations.

    “Dismantle and abolish ICE.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    The measure is enforcing immigration law and deporting those who break it. This removes the agency that does it.

    Source: Troy Jackson for Maine, Troy's priorities in the SenateDiscuss this
  • October 2026 · His campaign siteCapitalism

    Puts Medicare for All, government health insurance for every American, first on his list of priorities.

    “Pass Medicare for all.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    The measure is free markets. Government health insurance for everyone is a large new government program, the other direction.

    Source: Troy Jackson for Maine, Troy's priorities in the SenateDiscuss this
  • October 2026 · His campaign siteCapitalism

    Lists among his priorities both higher taxes on billionaires and corporations and tax cuts for working-class people.

    “Tax the billionaires and make sure greedy corporations pay their fair share. Deliver tax cuts for the working class.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    Lower taxes is the measure. He wants them lower for some and higher for others and names no bill, so it counts for nothing.

    Source: Troy Jackson for Maine, Troy's priorities in the SenateDiscuss this
  • April 17, 2026 · Candidate questionnaireFree speech

    Answered yes when Maine Public asked, during his run for governor, whether he supports the First Amendment, including speech and a press he might consider biased.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    The measure is no censorship by government. He says the protection covers speech he disagrees with.

    Source: Maine Public, Your Vote 2026 profile: Troy JacksonDiscuss this
  • April 17, 2026 · Candidate questionnaireAnti-woke

    Answered no when Maine Public asked, during his run for governor, whether he supports a ban on transgender athletes in girls' and women's sports and the dismantling of other protections under the Maine Human Rights Act.

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count a vote for the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act as with liberty. The question joined two things, and his answer to it was no.

    Source: Maine Public, Your Vote 2026 profile: Troy JacksonDiscuss this

    Lists among his priorities a federal civil rights law to protect lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender people from discrimination.

    “Pass comprehensive civil rights legislation protecting the LGBTQ+ community from discrimination.”

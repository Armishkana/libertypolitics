  • October 2026 · His campaign siteIran: the regime

    Condemned the American bombing of Iran and said talks in Geneva should have been chosen over strikes.

    “real strength is the courage to choose diplomacy when the easy path is violence.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Criticising the strikes on the regime and calling for a diplomatic route instead is opposing action against Iran.

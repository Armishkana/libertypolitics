John E. Sununu, on the record

Sununu has no votes in Congress since 2021, so there is no letter grade. This is what Sununu has said and done on our measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes.

  • June 22, 2026 · News reportIran: the regime

    Opposed the 300 billion dollars in American reconstruction aid for Iran that was part of the administration's proposed deal to end the war.

    “I don’t think the U.S. should provide any money for reconstruction”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    The measure is no deal that lets the regime survive. He opposes American money to rebuild it.

    Source: New Hampshire Public Radio, Senate candidates in NH criticize Trump's Iran dealReport this entry
  • August 27, 2026 · InterviewIran: the regime

    Said in a radio interview that it was a mistake to go to war with Iran without a plan to protect the Strait of Hormuz, and that he does not support sending ground troops.

    “I certainly said that it was a mistake to go in in the first place without having a plan for protecting the Strait of Hormuz.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    He faults how the war began, and in the same interview he wants a policy that keeps Iran from a nuclear weapon. He has not said he would vote to stop the war, so it counts for nothing.

    Source: New Hampshire Public Radio, Primary conversations: John E. Sununu, transcriptReport this entry

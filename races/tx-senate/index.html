Ken Paxton, on the record

Paxton has no votes in Congress since 2021, so the grade above comes from this list: what Paxton has said and done on our measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes.

  • March 2026 · News reportIran: the regime

    Days after the United States began strikes on Iran, told Fox News that the president did the right thing.

    “But the reality is, when you've got a country that's trying to build nuclear weapons, that is willing to use them, and that has demonstrated terrorist activity for decades, 40 or 50 years, you’ve got to deal with that, or eventually it comes to you”
    By the test we use for votes

    The measure is maximum pressure on the regime in Tehran. We count a vote to stop American action against it as against liberty; he backed the action.

    Told Bloomberg in September 2026 that he would have preferred the strikes on Iran to come after the midterm elections, and that the president had no choice if Iran was close to a nuclear weapon.

    “But the reality is, it sounds like he didn’t have a choice if he wanted to protect the country from an Iran with nuclear weapons”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    He would have timed it differently, and he still backs the action against the regime. The measure is no limits on America's power to act against it.

    Source: Texas Tribune, Paxton, Talarico spar over gas tax suspension amid rising pricesDiscuss this

    Told Fox News that the president is encouraging the people of Iran to take their country back, and that the United States would not move in to help them do it.

    “We're just taking out the bad guys, and then it's up to them to build their country in a way that they see fit.”
    By the test we use for votes

    With liberty

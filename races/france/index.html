What France votes on next

  • Apr 182027

    President, first round

    Voters in Guadeloupe, Martinique, French Guiana, Saint-Pierre-et-Miquelon, Saint-Barthélemy, Saint-Martin and French Polynesia vote the day before, Saturday 17 April.

    Which two candidates go through to the second round for President of the Republic, unless one wins outright.

    All French citizens on the electoral roll vote for one candidate. To stand, a candidate must be sponsored by 500 elected officials. A candidate with more than half of the votes cast is elected at once; otherwise the two with the most votes meet two weeks later. President Macron cannot stand, because nobody may serve more than two terms in a row.

    Source: Service-Public.fr, Élection 2027 : les dates de l'élection présidentielleSource: Conseil constitutionnel, Texte intégral de la Constitution du 4 octobre 1958 en vigueur
  • May 22027

    President, second round

    Held only if nobody wins more than half of the votes in the first round; the same overseas territories vote on Saturday 1 May.

    The President of the Republic, elected for five years.

    Only the two candidates who came first in the first round may stand, and the one with more votes wins. The president is elected for five years by direct vote.

    Source: Service-Public.fr, Élection 2027 : les dates de l'élection présidentielleSource: Conseil constitutionnel, Texte intégral de la Constitution du 4 octobre 1958 en vigueur

Matt Day, on the record

I have no votes in Congress to count for Day, so the grade above comes from this list: what Day has said and done on my measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes.

  • October 2026 · His campaign siteIsrael

    His revenue plan would require nations such as Israel that receive US military aid to hand over a share of technology export revenue.

    “Nations like Israel and Taiwan receiving military aid must allocate a share of future revenue from technology exports”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Attaching a payment condition to military aid for Israel is conditioning aid, the against side of the Israel measure, though his reason is money and not politics.

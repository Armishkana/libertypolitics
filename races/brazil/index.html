What Brazil voted on, and votes on next

  • Oct 252026

    President, second round Held

    Result: Lula won the second round on 25 October 2026 with 51.79% of the valid votes to 48.21% for Flávio Bolsonaro, and begins a fourth term in January 2027.

    Who is president of Brazil for the four years that begin in January 2027.

    Voters across the country choose between the two candidates who got the most votes in the first round on 4 October 2026, because nobody won more than half of the valid votes that day. The one with more valid votes on 25 October wins and takes office in January 2027.

    Source: Agência Brasil, Quando será o segundo turno das eleições? Veja a dataSource: Agência Brasil, Quando será o segundo turno das eleições? Veja a dataResult: InfoMoney, Eleições 2026: Lula é eleito presidente do Brasil; veja o resultado nos estados
  • Oct 252026

    Governors, second round in five states and the Federal District Held

    Result: Mailza Assis (PP) won in Acre, Omar Aziz (PSD) in Amazonas, Lorenzo Pazolini (Republicanos) in Espírito Santo, Cadu de Lula (PT) in Rio Grande do Norte, Vicentinho Júnior (PSDB) in Tocantins and Celina Leão (PP) in the Federal District.

    Rio de Janeiro did not vote again: on 8 October 2026 the election court voided the third-placed candidate's votes, which gave Douglas Ruas (PL) more than half of the valid votes in the first round.

    The governors of Acre, Amazonas, Espírito Santo, Rio Grande do Norte and Tocantins, and of the Federal District.

    Where no candidate for governor won more than half of the valid votes on 4 October 2026, voters in that state choose between the top two on the same day as the presidential second round. The other 20 states elected their governors in the first round.

    Source: Agência Brasil, Seis estados e o DF terão segundo turno para governadorSource: Agência Brasil, Seis estados e o DF terão segundo turno para governadorResult: InfoMoney, Eleições 2026: Lula é eleito presidente do Brasil; veja o resultado nos estados
  • Oct 12028

    Mayors and city councils

    The mayor and the city council of every one of Brazil's municipalities, for four years from January 2029.

    The constitution sets the vote for the first Sunday of October, every four years, in every municipality on the same day. In cities with more than 200,000 voters a mayor needs more than half of the valid votes, or the top two meet again on the last Sunday of October.

    Source: Planalto, Constituição da República Federativa do Brasil de 1988Source: Planalto, Constituição da República Federativa do Brasil de 1988
  • Oct 62030

    General election (president, Congress, governors)

    The president for the four years from January 2031, the Chamber of Deputies, one third of the Senate, every governor and every state assembly.

    The constitution sets the first round on the first Sunday of October and a second round on the last Sunday if no candidate for president or governor wins more than half of the valid votes. Lula cannot stand: a president may be re-elected only once in a row.

    Source: Planalto, Constituição da República Federativa do Brasil de 1988Source: Planalto, Constituição da República Federativa do Brasil de 1988

What Brazil votes on next

  • Oct 252026

    President, second round

    Who is president of Brazil for the four years that begin in January 2027.

    Voters across the country choose between the two candidates who got the most votes in the first round on 4 October 2026, because nobody won more than half of the valid votes that day. The one with more valid votes on 25 October wins and takes office in January 2027.

    Source: Agência Brasil, Quando será o segundo turno das eleições? Veja a dataSource: Agência Brasil, Quando será o segundo turno das eleições? Veja a data
  • Oct 252026

    Governors, second round in six states and the Federal District

    In Rio de Janeiro the second round may not be held: the third-placed candidate dropped his appeal on 5 October 2026, and if the election court voids his votes the leader passes 50% of the valid votes.

    The governors of Acre, Amazonas, Espírito Santo, Rio de Janeiro, Rio Grande do Norte and Tocantins, and of the Federal District.

    Where no candidate for governor won more than half of the valid votes on 4 October 2026, voters in that state choose between the top two on the same day as the presidential second round. The other 20 states elected their governors in the first round.

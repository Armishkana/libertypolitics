Fred Wellman, on the record

I have no votes in Congress to count for Wellman, so the grade above comes from this list: what Wellman has said and done on my measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test I use for votes.

  • October 2026 · His campaign siteIran: the regime

    He calls the Iran war a disaster and says Congress must act to end it.

    “The Iran war has been an unmitigated disaster in every way. Congress must immediately act to end this misbegotten venture”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Condemning the war on Iran and calling for Congress to end it is the against side of the iran_regime measure.

    Source: Wellman for Missouri, Fred on the issuesDiscuss this
  • October 2026 · His campaign siteIsrael

    He backs the Stop the Bombs Act and says US military aid to Israel must follow American and international law.

    “The killing must end, humanitarian aid must reach civilians, and the United States must ensure that our military assistance is used in accordance with American and international law. I support the Stop the Bombs Act, the West Bank Violence Prevention Act, and enforcement of the Leahy Law.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    Conditioning military aid to Israel on law and backing the Stop the Bombs Act is the against side of the israel measure.

    Source: Wellman for Missouri, Fred on the issuesDiscuss this
  • October 2026 · His campaign siteAllies

    He supports continued military aid to Ukraine and a strong NATO.

    “I support continued military assistance to help Ukraine defend itself and a strong, united NATO capable of deterring further Russian aggression.”
    By the test I use for votes
    AWith liberty

    Backing Ukraine against Russia and backing NATO is the with side of the allies measure.

    Source: Wellman for Missouri, Fred on the issuesDiscuss this

    He says a secure border is reasonable but calls the current immigration system broken, without naming a policy.

    “It is reasonable to expect a secure border, but our current system is broken, costly, and keeps hardworking people from contributing to our communities and economy.”

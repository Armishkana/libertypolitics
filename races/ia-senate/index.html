Josh Turek, on the record

Turek has no votes in Congress since 2021, so there is no letter grade. This is what Turek has said and done on our measures. Each line has its source, and each is marked with the same test we use for votes.

  • February 28, 2026 · News reportIran: the regime

    On the day the United States began airstrikes on Iran, said at a forum in Council Bluffs that Iran should not have nuclear weapons and that he did not want a forever war.

    “We do not want Iran to have nuclear weapons. However, I want to make sure that we don't end up in forever wars and forever conflicts as well.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    The measure is maximum pressure on the regime in Tehran. On the first day he neither backed the strikes nor opposed them, so this line counts for nothing.

    Source: The Gazette, Iowa Democratic Senate candidate Josh Turek says he wants to avoid 'forever wars'Report this entry
  • May 5, 2026 · DebateIran: the regime

    Asked in the Democratic primary debate whether he would vote to go to war, called the war in Iran one of the forever wars and said he would be against it.

    “I would be fundamentally against the war.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count a vote to stop American action against the regime as against liberty. He says he would oppose the war.

    Source: Iowa PBS, Iowa Press Debates: U.S. Senate Democratic Primary (transcript)Report this entry
  • February 28, 2026 · His campaign siteIran: the people

    In his statement on the airstrikes, called the Iranian regime brutal and repressive and said bombing would not bring lasting change to the country.

    “While there is no question the Iranian regime is brutal and repressive, bombing Iran is not the same thing as bringing lasting change to the country.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    The measure is backing the people of Iran against their rulers. He condemns the regime and names no step to help the people, so it settles nothing and counts for nothing.

    Source: Josh Turek for Iowa, statement on military action in IranReport this entry
  • February 13, 2025 · Vote in the Iowa HouseIsrael

    Voted in the Iowa House for a resolution expressing support for Israel and condemning antisemitism, after voting for an amendment, which failed, that would have made that support conditional on Israel following humanitarian law.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    We count a vote for a resolution standing with Israel as with liberty. The amendment he backed first would have put conditions on that support.

    Source: Jewish Insider, Leading Democratic Senate recruit in Iowa backed conditions on U.S. support for IsraelReport this entry
  • October 1, 2026 · News reportIsrael

    Told Iowa Starting Line that no American tax money should go to any organization, group or regime that does not abide by international law, and has called Israel one of the few functioning democracies in the Middle East.

    “My thought is that there should be no taxpayer dollars going to any organization, group, regime that is not abiding by international law”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    We count a vote to block weapons for Israel as against liberty. He sets a condition on aid and does not say here whether he would block any, so it does not settle the question and counts for nothing.

    Source: Newsweek, Troy Jackson Apologizes to Jewish Mainers: What Other Democrats Have SaidReport this entry
  • February 2026 · His campaign siteMilitary

    His platform pledges support for troops, military families and veterans; it does not say what he would do on the defense budget.

    “Support our military heroes and their families while they serve – and when they come home.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    Care for troops and veterans is not the same question as funding and equipping the forces, so this counts for nothing.

    Source: Josh Turek for Iowa, Pushing for Change: Josh Turek's PlatformReport this entry
  • May 5, 2026 · DebateMilitary

    Said in the Democratic primary debate that spending 1.5 trillion dollars on what he called the military industrial complex is a misuse of resources, and that Social Security should be protected first.

    “we're willing to spend $1.5 trillion for military industrial complex. I think that it is a, it's a misuse of our resources”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    The measure is funding and equipping the armed forces. We count a vote for the yearly defense bill as with liberty, and a vote to cut the defense budget as against.

    Source: Iowa PBS, Iowa Press Debates: U.S. Senate Democratic Primary (transcript)Report this entry
  • March 19, 2024 · Vote in the Iowa HouseBorders

    Was one of the Democrats who joined Republicans in the Iowa House to pass the 2024 bill that makes it a state crime to be in the country illegally after being deported or denied entry.

    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    The measure is enforcing immigration law and deporting those who break it. The bill lets Iowa police arrest people who came back after being deported.

    Source: The Gazette, Lawmakers OK bill allowing Iowa cops to arrest people in U.S. illegallyReport this entry
  • May 5, 2026 · DebateBorders

    Said in the Democratic primary debate that he would not have voted to fund the Department of Homeland Security without changes to Immigration and Customs Enforcement, such as no masks, body cameras and limits near churches and schools.

    “I would have not supported any DHS funding without serious reforms to ICE.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    The measure is enforcing immigration law. We count a vote to fund border and immigration enforcement as with liberty; he says he would have withheld that vote.

    Source: Iowa PBS, Iowa Press Debates: U.S. Senate Democratic Primary (transcript)Report this entry
  • February 2026 · His campaign siteCapitalism

    His platform calls for a federal ban on what it calls price gouging on food.

    “Impose a federal ban on price gouging on food so Iowans pay less at the grocery store.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    The measure is free markets with no price controls. A federal limit on what food may cost is a price control.

    Source: Josh Turek for Iowa, Pushing for Change: Josh Turek's PlatformReport this entry
  • February 2026 · His campaign siteCapitalism

    His platform pledges tax cuts for the middle class and working families together with closing tax loopholes for billionaires and large companies.

    “Cut taxes for the middle class and working families and close loopholes for billionaires and big corporations.”
    By the test we use for votes
    Not clear either way

    The measure is lower taxes. He would lower some and raise others, and names no bill, so this does not settle it and counts for nothing.

    Source: Josh Turek for Iowa, Pushing for Change: Josh Turek's PlatformReport this entry
  • February 2026 · His campaign siteFree speech

    His platform pledges to protect freedom of speech and protest.

    “Protect the freedom of speech and protest.”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    The measure is no censorship by government. This is that.

    Source: Josh Turek for Iowa, Pushing for Change: Josh Turek's PlatformReport this entry
  • February 2026 · His campaign siteAnti-woke

    His campaign site says he spoke out against the legislation that took gender identity out of Iowa's civil rights code.

    By the test we use for votes
    Against liberty

    We count a vote against writing gender identity into federal civil rights law as with liberty. He argued for keeping it in Iowa law.

    Source: Josh Turek for Iowa, Pushing for Change: Josh Turek's PlatformReport this entry
  • July 2026 · News reportAnti-woke

    Told The New York Times that he does not think biological males should compete against females in sports.

    “As someone that spent a lot of my life in high-level sports, I don’t believe that biological males should compete against females in sports.”
    By the test we use for votes
    With liberty

    We count a vote for the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act as with liberty.

    Source: Roll Call, As GOP revives anti-trans playbook, Dems try to turn the pageReport this entry

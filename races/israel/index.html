      What Israel voted on, and votes on next

      • Oct 272026

        Knesset (parliament), 26th Held

        Result: Likud came first with 27 of the 120 seats and Yashar second with 21 when the Central Elections Committee finished the count on 29 October 2026. Netanyahu's bloc has 53 seats, Eisenkot's bloc 48 and the two Arab parties 19, so no bloc has the 61 needed; the official results are due in early November 2026.

        Fills all 120 seats of the Knesset, the single house of parliament, which then decides who forms the government.

        The whole country is one district and each voter casts one vote for a party list, not for a person. Seats are shared out by each list's share of the vote, and a list needs 3.25 percent to get any. No party has ever won a majority alone, so after the vote the president asks the leader best placed to gather 61 of the 120 members to form a coalition.

        Source: The Jerusalem Post, Israel sets election date for October 27Source: Ynetnews, Israel heads to election on October 27 after Knesset dissolves itselfResult: Israel National News, Final Results: 53 seats for Netanyahu bloc, 48 seats for opposition

        Knesset (parliament), 27th

        Due by law in the Hebrew month of Cheshvan (October or November) of 2030, four years after the 2026 vote, unless the Knesset is dissolved earlier.

        Fills all 120 seats of the Knesset, the single house of parliament, which then decides who forms the government.

        The Knesset sits for four years from the day it is elected. When the last Knesset dissolved itself, as it did before the 2026 vote, the next one sits until the month of Cheshvan after those four years, and the vote falls on a Tuesday of that month. The Knesset can dissolve itself and call an earlier vote.

        Source: Constitute Project, Israel's Basic Laws (rev. 2013), Basic Law: The KnessetSource: Constitute Project, Israel's Basic Laws (rev. 2013), Basic Law: The Knesset

Matthew Dunlap, on the record

  • April 2026 · StatementIran: the regime

    Asked about Congress and war on Iran, he said he would support a war powers resolution calling on the president to end the conflict and seek congressional approval.

    “I'll support the War Powers Resolution in Congress calling on the President to end this conflict and seek congressional approval.”
    By the test I use for votes
    FAgainst liberty

    A war powers resolution aimed at stopping action against Iran is on the against side of the Iran regime measure.

    FAgainst liberty

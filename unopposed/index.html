Unopposed

On November 3, 65% of the seats on the ballot in the 21 states counted so far had only one name on it, or none. Nobody to choose between.

33,887 seats counted from each state's official results, 21 states in full and 11 in part. 21,931 had no race. Find your county to see every seat that was on your ballot.

      How this is counted

      • Every contest comes from the state's own official results for the November 3, 2026 general election: the names printed on the ballot, and how many people each contest elects.
      • A seat had a race when more people were on the ballot than there were seats. It had no race when there were only as many names as seats, or fewer. A seat with no name at all is an empty chair.
      • Write-in lines are not counted as candidates. Ballot measures are left out. A yes-or-no vote on keeping a judge has no opponent by design, so it is shown but never counted in the share.
      • A district that crosses county lines (a seat in Congress, a judicial district) is listed in each county where it was on the ballot, and counted once in a state's total.
      • Some seats are elected in spring or in odd years, so they are not on this list. Results can still change before they are certified; the page is read again as states certify.

      Where every number comes from

      • Alabama: Alabama Secretary of State
      • California: California Secretary of State (election-night results API), California Secretary of State: API endpoint list for the 3 Nov 2026 general (districts by county). Not counted: County, city, school board, superior court judge and special district races, which each of California's 58 counties reports on its own website.
      • Colorado: Colorado Secretary of State (Clarity results, every county site)
      • Connecticut: Connecticut Secretary of the State, Election Management System. Not counted: Nine town contests whose number of seats neither the state feed nor the ballot gives (under Connecticut's minority-representation rules a voter may vote for fewer people than there are seats): the boards of education in Berlin, Darien and Stamford and the six Darien Representative Town Meeting districts.
      • Delaware: Delaware Department of Elections
      • Georgia: Georgia Secretary of State
      • Hawaii: Hawaii Office of Elections: county Summary Reports, 2026 General
      • Illinois: Illinois county clerks: election night results (Clarity). Not counted: Only DuPage, McHenry, Macoupin and Madison counties are counted. Illinois has no statewide results feed, and the other 98 county clerks (and the Chicago board) each post their results on their own websites.
      • Indiana: Indiana Election Division: election night results
      • Iowa: Iowa Secretary of State: election results (Clarity)
      • Kansas: Kansas Secretary of State: Candidate List, 2026 General, U.S. Census Bureau: 2024 state legislative district to county relationship files. Not counted: U.S. House and State Board of Education races (no official list of the counties in each district was read), district court and magistrate judges, and every county and local office, which each county election officer handles.
      • Louisiana: Louisiana Secretary of State: Candidate Inquiry (11/03/2026) In Louisiana a candidate with no opponent is declared elected when qualifying closes and is never printed on the ballot. They are counted here as unopposed, from the Secretary of State's list of qualified candidates.
      • Maryland: Maryland State Board of Elections
      • Minnesota: Minnesota Secretary of State: election results media files, Minnesota Secretary of State: election results
      • Missouri: Missouri Secretary of State: Certification of Candidates, General Election November 3, 2026, Missouri Secretary of State: Official Manual 2025-2026, Judicial Branch (Court of Appeals districts), U.S. Census Bureau: 2024 state legislative district to county relationship files. Not counted: U.S. House races (the districts were redrawn in 2025 and no official county list of the new districts was read), circuit and associate circuit judges, and every county office, which each county clerk certifies.
      • Montana: Montana Secretary of State, election results. Not counted: Conservation district supervisors and one town selectman (Pinesdale), because the state results site does not print how many seats each of those contests fills. School boards and city councils are elected in May and odd years, not in November.
      • Nebraska: Nebraska Secretary of State: Election Results. Not counted: County offices (commissioners or supervisors, clerk, sheriff, attorney and the rest), city and village councils and school boards, which each county election office reports on its own.
      • New Jersey: New Jersey county clerks (Clarity ENR, each county site), New Jersey Division of Elections: Official List of Candidates, U.S. Senate and House (general, 3 Nov 2026). Not counted: County, town and school board races, which each New Jersey county clerk reports on its own site. Full ballots are read only for Atlantic and Essex counties; elsewhere only U.S. Senate and House are counted.
      • New York: Board of Elections in the City of New York. Not counted: Only New York City's five counties are counted. The other 57 counties are not, because the state results site refused automated reads after the election and each county board reports on its own site.
      • North Carolina: NC State Board of Elections
      • North Dakota: North Dakota Secretary of State, election night results
      • Oregon: Oregon Secretary of State (results.oregonvotes.gov), county exports
      • Rhode Island: Rhode Island Board of Elections
      • South Carolina: SC Election Commission
      • South Dakota: South Dakota Secretary of State, election results. Not counted: South Dakota law (SDCL 12-16-1.1) leaves a county or city candidate with no opponent off the ballot and declares them elected, so unopposed county and city seats were never on the ballot and are not counted here; school boards and most city councils are elected in spring, not in November.
      • Texas: Texas Secretary of State (election night results). Not counted: County offices (county judge, commissioners, clerks, treasurer, justices of the peace, constables), city and school board races, which each Texas county reports on its own site.
      • Utah: Utah Lieutenant Governor (election results, every county)
      • Vermont: Vermont Secretary of State: Election Night Results. Not counted: Justice of the peace races in 103 of 247 towns, which those towns had not posted to the state results site when it was read.
      • Virginia: Virginia Department of Elections
      • Washington: Washington Secretary of State (results.votewa.gov)
      • West Virginia: West Virginia Secretary of State (Clarity ENR, every county site)
      • Wyoming: Wyoming Secretary of State, unofficial precinct-by-precinct summaries, Wyoming Secretary of State, 2026 general election candidate list. Not counted: County offices (commissioners, clerk, sheriff and the rest), city and town councils, school boards and special districts, which each Wyoming county clerk reports on the county's own website.

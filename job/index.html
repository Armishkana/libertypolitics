The jobDiscussion

Run the screen for a live show

Liberty Politics is a daily live panel show on YouTube. It's hiring one person to run the screen, from home. The panel does the talking. You run the show.

Pay
500 USD a month, after a two week paid trial at 125 USD a week
Hours
About 5 hours a day, six days a week, two shows a day
Show times
11am to 2pm and 3pm to 5pm, U.S. Eastern time.
Where
From home
Apply with two short videos

What you'd do

  • Line up the news posts before the show, from a written list of rules.
  • Share your screen and change the post when it's time to move on.
  • Put up the banners, the Super Chat goal and the polls.
  • Show viewer comments on screen and make sure Super Chats get read.
  • Let guests in and check their camera and microphone.
  • Keep the show on time.

It all runs in StreamYard, in a browser. Full training is provided.

What it takes

  • Being reliable at the same hours every day.
  • Being calm and quick with a browser, tabs and a live chat.
  • Good written and spoken English.
  • Following the news, or wanting to.
  • Stable internet and a quiet place to work.

You don't give political opinions on air. On some days you may be on camera in a corner of the screen.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the form. It asks for two short videos: about 2 minutes of your screen while you run a test broadcast in StreamYard (a free account is enough), and 30 seconds of you on camera saying hello. Applications without the videos aren't read.
  2. Armin Navabi, who hosts the show, watches every video.
  3. The people who fit get an email back. The videos come first for everyone, so please don't ask for a call before them.
  4. A yes starts the two week paid trial.

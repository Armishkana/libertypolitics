Help build it

Liberty Score is a group project. We built the first version in October 2026. Making it right is a job for everyone who uses it.

539 politicians and 228 votes are more than one team can get right alone. The votes are checked against the official record. The summaries, the choice of votes and the writing are where you come in, and you know your own representative better than we do.

What helps most

  • A statement, an interview or a state vote by a candidate we have not researched, with a link.
  • A vote we recorded wrong, or a link that goes to the wrong place.
  • A summary of a bill that is unfair, unclear or out of date.
  • A vote we should be counting and are not.
  • Anything that breaks or looks wrong on your phone.
  • A sentence that is confusing, or that made you wince.

What happens to your note

  1. You write it in the box at the bottom of any page. The yellow button in the corner takes you there. No account and no email.
  2. We check it. A claim about a vote is checked against the official roll call. A bug is tested on a phone and on a computer.
  3. If you are right, we fix it and list the fix on this page, with your name if you gave one.
  4. If it is a judgment call, such as which side of a vote is the liberty side, the hosts decide.

What will not change a grade

Volume. Ten notes saying a senator deserves an A change nothing. One note showing a vote we got wrong changes the grade as soon as we confirm it.

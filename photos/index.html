Liberty Score is made by Liberty Politics, a daily live show and a community.

Grades come from recorded votes and cosponsored bills in the 117th, 118th and 119th Congress, counted through September 30, 2026. Last updated October 4, 2026.

Liberty Score grades voting records. It does not endorse or oppose any candidate for office, and it is not connected to any party, campaign or committee. Portraits of members of Congress are official congressional photographs. Photographs of other candidates are from Wikimedia Commons, cropped, and each is credited on the photo credits page. Vote data: Voteview, the Clerk of the House, the Secretary of the Senate and Congress.gov. Candidate lists: the state election offices, as compiled on Wikipedia, checked against the Federal Election Commission.

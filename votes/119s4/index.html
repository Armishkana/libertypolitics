Laken Riley Act amendment: remove states' power to sue

Senate vote · S. 5 · On the Amendment · January 15, 2025Borders

Would have removed the part of the Laken Riley Act that lets state attorneys general sue federal officials who fail to detain illegal immigrants.

Liberty position
Nay
The Senate voted
46 Yea, 49 Nay
Why Nay is the liberty side
Voting no keeps a tool for forcing enforcement.

Counted under the principle Citizenship and the rule of law.

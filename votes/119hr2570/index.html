Maximum Pressure Act

Bill sponsorship · H.R. 2570Iran: the regime

Writes the maximum pressure sanctions on Iran into law, sanctions the Supreme Leader, and keeps all sanctions in place until the regime meets twelve demands on its nuclear, missile and terrorism programs.

Liberty position
Sign on
Introduced

Why signing on is the liberty side
It is the maximum pressure policy in bill form.

Expressing support for the Iranian people's desire for a democratic, secular, and nonnuclear Republic of Iran

Bill sponsorship · H.Res. 100Iran: the people

Condemns the regime's terrorism and killing of protesters, cites support for the ten-point plan of Maryam Rajavi, leader of the MEK-linked National Council of Resistance of Iran, and says Iranians are 'rejecting monarchic dictatorship and religious tyranny'.

Liberty position
Stay off
Introduced
February 7, 2023
Why staying off is the liberty side
The text backs the ten-point plan of Maryam Rajavi, who leads the political arm of the MEK, and says Iranians have rejected the monarchy. It takes one exile group's side on who should lead after the regime, against Prince Reza Pahlavi. So signing it counts against.

Counted under the measure Stands with the Iranian people.

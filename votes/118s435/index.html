Hagerty motion: count only citizens for House seats

Senate vote · H.R. 4366 · On the Motion · March 8, 2024Borders

Would have added a requirement that the census count citizenship and that only citizens be counted when dividing House seats.

Liberty position
Yea
The Senate voted
45 Yea, 51 Nay
Why Yea is the liberty side
It stops illegal immigration from adding House seats to states with large non-citizen populations.

Counted under the measure Stops mass migration.

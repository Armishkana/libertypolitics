Iran Human Rights, Internet Freedom, and Accountability Act of 2026

Bill sponsorship · H.R. 7622Iran: the people

Expands internet access for Iranians during regime blackouts, enforces sanctions on human rights abusers, and funds broadcasting and online security tools for Iranian activists and journalists.

Liberty position
Sign on
Introduced
February 20, 2026
Why signing on is the liberty side
It gives practical help to Iranians standing up to the regime.

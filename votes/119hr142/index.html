Regulations from the Executive in Need of Scrutiny (REINS) Act of 2025

Bill sponsorship · H.R. 142Free markets

Requires Congress to approve any major federal regulation before it can take effect.

Liberty position
Sign on
Introduced
January 3, 2025
Why signing on is the liberty side
It is the main bill to rein in federal regulation, and it has had no stand-alone House vote in the 119th Congress.

Counted under the principle Free markets and free trade.

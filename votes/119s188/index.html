Free Speech Protection Act

Bill sponsorship · S. 188Free speech

Bars federal employees and contractors from directing online platforms to censor speech protected by the First Amendment.

Liberty position
Sign on
Introduced
January 22, 2025
Why signing on is the liberty side
The Senate has had no vote on government pressure on platforms, so cosponsorship is the best signal.

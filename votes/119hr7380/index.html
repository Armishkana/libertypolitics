IRAN Act (Internet Reach and Access Now Act)

Bill sponsorship · H.R. 7380Iran: the people

Makes the State Department responsible for getting Iranians around the regime's internet shutdowns, including satellite and direct-to-phone service, and clears sanctions obstacles to those tools.

Liberty position
Sign on
Introduced
February 4, 2026
Why signing on is the liberty side
It helps Iranians stay online when the regime cuts them off.

Counted under the measure Stands with the Iranian people.

Iran Human Rights, Internet Freedom, and Accountability Act of 2026

Bill sponsorship · S. 3900Iran: the people

Senate version. Expands internet access for Iranians during regime blackouts, enforces sanctions on human rights abusers, and funds broadcasting and online security tools for Iranian activists and journalists.

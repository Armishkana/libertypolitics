NO BAN Act

Bill sponsorship · H.R. 924Islamic migration

Would limit the president's power to suspend entry of foreign nationals and bar religion-based entry restrictions, aimed at the travel bans on Muslim-majority countries.

Liberty position
Stay off
Introduced
February 4, 2025
Why staying off is the liberty side
Same bill as the 2021 House vote. Cosponsoring it means opposing travel restrictions on high-risk Islamic countries.

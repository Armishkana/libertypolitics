FREEDOM Act (Feasibility Review of Emerging Equipment for Digital Open Media Act)

Bill sponsorship · H.R. 6469Iran: the people

Requires a State Department report on internet freedom in Iran and on new technology, such as direct-to-phone satellite service, that could get Iranians past censorship.

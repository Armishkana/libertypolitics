About Liberty Politics

Liberty Politics is a daily live show and a community. We cover U.S. politics, Israel, Iran and the rest of the world, live, with a panel. Then the people watching get their turn.

Why we built a scorecard

A politician can say anything. A vote is on the record for good.

We wanted one place where anyone can look up their own senator or representative and see, vote by vote, what they actually did. We could not find one that asked the questions we care about. So we built it.

Where we stand

A scorecard needs a standard, and ours is not neutral. We think liberty has to be defended at home and abroad at the same time.

At home, that means free speech, free markets, a secure border and no religious law.

Abroad, it means standing with people who are fighting for the freedoms Americans already have. We want the Islamic Republic gone, and we back the Iranian people and Prince Reza Pahlavi, Iran's exiled crown prince. We back Israel. We back Ukraine against Putin.

We are against the Islamization of the West, against communism and the radical left, and against isolationism, whether it comes from the left or from the right.

If you disagree with some of that, you are still welcome here. Bring your argument.

Who runs it

Armin Navabi hosts the show. He grew up in Iran under the Islamic Republic, and for twenty years he could not say out loud what he thought. That is where the standard on this site comes from.

He hosts from Vancouver, Canada, with co-hosts and a panel of people who started out as viewers.

The hosts and the panel do not share a religion or a party. Atheists, Christians, Hindus and Jews are all part of it.

Three ways in

  • Join the community. A group call every day with the hosts and the panel. This is where the arguments about these grades happen.
  • Watch live. The show is live on YouTube every day. Subscribe to know when.
  • Get on the panel. Panelists speak to a live audience and can point viewers to their own channel. Any politics welcome. You apply with a short video, one to three minutes.

Who pays for it

No party, campaign or network pays for Liberty Politics. Viewers and members do. If you want more of this, a membership in the community is how to back it today.

Disagree with a grade? Tell the hosts on a call.

The show is free to watch. The community is where you get to answer back, every day, with the hosts and the panel.

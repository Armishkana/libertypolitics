About Liberty Politics

We are not a news network. We amplify the opinions of the voiceless, and we unite patriots of every faith and none in one front against the enemies of liberty.

What we are for

  • The end of the Islamic Republic.
  • The Iranian people, and Prince Reza Pahlavi as their leader.
  • Israel.
  • A strong America that stands by its allies.
  • Ukraine, against Putin.
  • Free speech, and people who disagree with us in the room.

What we are against

  • The Islamic Republic, and everyone who speaks for it.
  • The Islamization of the West.
  • Communism and the radical left.
  • Isolationism, on the left and on the right.
  • Tyranny, anywhere in the world.

Who we are

Liberty Politics is led by Armin Navabi.

Armin grew up in Iran, under the Islamic Republic. For twenty years he could not say what he thought. He was an atheist under Islamic rule, and he says it felt like living in a small cage.

He made himself two promises. He would get free. And he would help more people get heard.

Liberty Politics is that promise, for everybody. Armin hosts the live shows from Vancouver, Canada, with co-hosts and a panel of people who came out of the community.

Atheists, Christians, Hindus and Jews sit at the same table here. We do not agree on everything. We agree on liberty.

Three ways in

  • Join the community. Daily group calls with the hosts and the panel. This is where you stop watching and start talking.
  • Watch live. Live discussions on U.S. politics, Israel, Iran, the Middle East and world affairs. Subscribe so you know when we go live.
  • Get on the panel. You get a live audience, and your own channel plugged on air. Any politics welcome. Camera on. Apply with a short video, one to three minutes.

Keep us on the air

We answer to viewers, not to a network. Donations open soon. Today, the way to back the show is a membership in our community.

How to vote in Arkansas

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: Arkansas has no online registration. You register on paper. Get the paper form from the state.
  • By mail: your form has to be postmarked by Monday, October 5.
  • In person: by Monday, October 5.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division.

To register, or to check that you are registered, start at Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division.

Source: Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division. Checked October 12, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Monday, October 19 to Monday, November 2.

Open 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Monday to Friday and 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday. It ends at 5 p.m. on the Monday before Election Day.

Source: Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division. Checked October 12, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Only with a reason the state accepts. Arkansas lists the reasons on its own page.

  • Ask for your ballot: by Tuesday, October 27.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to arrive by 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3.

Where the source gives a rule and not a day, I counted the day from it. In its words: “By Mail or by Electronic Means: by 7 days before the election.” “By Mail: received at clerks office by 7:30 p.m.”

Source: Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division. Checked October 12, 2026.

What do I bring?

Bring a photo ID, such as a driver license, a U.S. passport, a military ID or a concealed handgun license. Without one you vote a provisional ballot.

Source: Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division. Checked October 12, 2026.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 7:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place through Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division.

Source: Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division. Checked October 12, 2026.

The last word

Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division has it, not me. If anything here differs from Arkansas Secretary of State, Elections Division, the state is right. I checked these pages on October 12, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in Arkansas, or ask the others in Arkansas how voting went for them.

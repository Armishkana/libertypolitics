How to vote in Washington

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: by Monday, October 26.
  • By mail: your form has to arrive by Monday, October 26.
  • In person: by Tuesday, November 3.

Missed the deadline? You can still register and vote on the same day, during early voting and on Election Day.

Register on the state's own form. Not sure you are registered? Look yourself up on the state's own lookup.

Source: Washington Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Friday, October 16 to Tuesday, November 3.

Source: Washington Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Yes, and you don't have to ask. Washington mails a ballot to every registered voter.

  • Send it back: your ballot has to be postmarked by Tuesday, November 3.

Source: Washington Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

What do I bring?

Most voters need no ID. Sign the envelope that comes with your ballot. If you registered without an ID, show one to your county elections office so your ballot counts.

Source: Washington Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • When the polls are open: For this one, go by the state's own page: Washington Secretary of State, Elections.
  • Where to go: look it up on the state's own lookup.

The last word

Washington Secretary of State, Elections has it, not me. If anything here differs from Washington Secretary of State, Elections, the state is right. I checked these pages on October 11, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in Washington, or ask the others in Washington how voting went for them.

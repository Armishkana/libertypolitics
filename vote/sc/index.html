How to vote in South Carolina

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: by Sunday, October 4.
  • By mail: For this one, go by the state's own page: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission).
  • In person: by Sunday, October 4.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission).

Register on the state's own form. Not sure you are registered? Look yourself up on the state's own lookup.

Source: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission). Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Monday, October 19 to Saturday, October 31.

Source: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission). Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Only with a reason the state accepts. South Carolina lists the reasons on its own page.

  • Ask for your ballot: your request has to arrive by Friday, October 23.
  • Send it back by: For this one, go by the state's own page: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission).

Ask for your ballot on the state's own form.

Source: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission). Checked October 11, 2026.

What do I bring?

Bring a photo ID such as a South Carolina driver's license or DMV ID, a U.S. passport, a military ID, or a South Carolina voter registration card with your photo.

Source: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission). Checked October 11, 2026.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place on the state's own lookup.

Source: SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission). Checked October 11, 2026.

The last word

SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission) has it, not me. If anything here differs from SC Votes (South Carolina Election Commission), the state is right. I checked these pages on October 11, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in South Carolina, or ask the others in South Carolina how voting went for them.

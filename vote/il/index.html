How to vote in Illinois

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

For this one, go by the state's own page: Illinois State Board of Elections.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person ends Monday, November 2.

Its first day: For this one, go by the state's own page: Illinois State Board of Elections.

Source: Illinois State Board of Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Who can vote by mail: For this one, go by the state's own page: Illinois State Board of Elections.

  • Ask for your ballot: by Thursday, October 29.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to be postmarked by Tuesday, November 3.

Source: Illinois State Board of Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

What do I bring?

For this one, go by the state's own page: Illinois State Board of Elections.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 6:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place through Illinois State Board of Elections.

Source: Illinois State Board of Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

The last word

Illinois State Board of Elections has it, not me. If anything here differs from Illinois State Board of Elections, the state is right. I checked these pages on October 11, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in Illinois, or ask the others in Illinois how voting went for them.

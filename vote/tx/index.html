How to vote in Texas

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: Texas has no online registration. You register on paper.
  • By mail: your form has to be postmarked by Monday, October 5.
  • In person: by Monday, October 5.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: VoteTexas.gov (Texas Secretary of State).

Not sure you are registered? Look yourself up on the state's own lookup.

Source: VoteTexas.gov (Texas Secretary of State). Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Monday, October 19 to Friday, October 30.

Source: the state's own page at sos.state.tx.us. Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Only with a reason the state accepts. Texas lists the reasons on its own page.

  • Ask for your ballot: your request has to arrive by Friday, October 23.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to be postmarked by Tuesday, November 3. It also has to reach the office by 5:00 p.m. on Wednesday, November 4.

Ask for your ballot on the state's own form.

Source: the state's own page at sos.state.tx.us. Checked October 11, 2026.

What do I bring?

For this one, go by the state's own page: VoteTexas.gov (Texas Secretary of State).

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place on the state's own lookup.

Source: VoteTexas.gov (Texas Secretary of State). Checked October 11, 2026.

The last word

VoteTexas.gov (Texas Secretary of State) has it, not me. If anything here differs from VoteTexas.gov (Texas Secretary of State), the state is right. I checked these pages on October 11, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in Texas, or ask the others in Texas how voting went for them.

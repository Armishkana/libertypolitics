How to vote in Michigan

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: by Monday, October 19.
  • By mail: your form has to be postmarked by Monday, October 19.
  • In person: by Tuesday, November 3.

Missed the deadline? You can still register and vote on the same day, during early voting and on Election Day. In the last 14 days and on Election Day you can only register in person at your local clerk's office, with proof of where you live. Be in line by 8 p.m.

To register, or to check that you are registered, start at Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote).

Where the source gives a rule and not a day, I counted the day from it. In its words: “If there are 15+ days before an election, voters can register online, by mail, or in person.” “Applications must be received by, or postmarked as sent to your local clerk's office at least 15 days before Election Day in order to be processed in time for the next election.” “Voters in line by 8 p.m. on Election Day have the right to register to vote in person at their local clerk’s office.”

Source: Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote); a second page of it. Checked October 12, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Saturday, October 24 to Sunday, November 1.

This is the required minimum of nine days. Your local clerk sets the places and hours and may offer more days.

Where the source gives a rule and not a day, I counted the day from it. In its words: “For statewide and federal elections, the mandatory early voting period takes place for a minimum of nine consecutive days, ending on the Sunday before an election.”

Source: Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote). Checked October 12, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Yes. Any registered voter in Michigan can vote by mail, and nobody asks you why.

  • Ask for your ballot: by Friday, October 30.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to arrive by 8:00 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3.

Where the source gives a rule and not a day, I counted the day from it. In its words: “Deadline: Online applications may be submitted until 5 p.m. the Friday before Election Day.” “Deadline: Mailed absentee ballots from all voters, except military and overseas voters, must be received by the voter's local clerk by 8 p.m. on Election Day.”

Source: Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote). Checked October 12, 2026.

What do I bring?

Show a photo ID if you have one. If you do not have it with you, you can sign a short form and still vote a regular ballot.

Source: Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote). Checked October 12, 2026.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place through Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote).

Source: Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote). Checked October 12, 2026.

The last word

Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote) has it, not me. If anything here differs from Michigan Secretary of State, Elections (Michigan.gov/Vote), the state is right. I checked these pages on October 12, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in Michigan, or ask the others in Michigan how voting went for them.

How to vote in New York

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: For this one, go by the state's own page: New York State Board of Elections.
  • By mail: your form has to arrive by Saturday, October 24.
  • In person: by Saturday, October 24.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: New York State Board of Elections.

Register on the state's own form.

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked October 12, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person runs from Saturday, October 24 to Sunday, November 1.

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked October 12, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Yes. Any registered voter in New York can vote by mail, and nobody asks you why.

  • Ask for your ballot: your request has to arrive by Saturday, October 24.
  • Send it back: your ballot has to be postmarked by Tuesday, November 3.

Where the source gives a rule and not a day, I counted the day from it. In its words: “Applications requesting to receive an early mail ballot must be received by the board of elections in your county no later than ten days before the election.”

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked October 12, 2026.

What do I bring?

Most voters do not show ID. If your identity could not be checked when you registered, you are asked for ID the first time you vote.

Source: New York State Board of Elections. Checked October 12, 2026.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place through New York State Board of Elections.

Source: New York Election Law 8-100. Checked October 12, 2026.

The last word

New York State Board of Elections has it, not me. If anything here differs from New York State Board of Elections, the state is right. I checked these pages on October 12, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in New York, or ask the others in New York how voting went for them.

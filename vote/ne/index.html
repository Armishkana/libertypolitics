How to vote in Nebraska

Five questions, in the order you will ask them. Every date links to the page it was read on.

Can I still register?

  • Online: For this one, go by the state's own page: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections.
  • By mail: For this one, go by the state's own page: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections.
  • In person: by Friday, October 23.

Same day registration: For this one, go by the state's own page: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections.

Register on the state's own form. Not sure you are registered? Look yourself up on the state's own lookup.

Source: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote early?

Yes. Early voting in person ends Monday, November 2.

Its first day: For this one, go by the state's own page: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections.

Vote in person at your county clerk or election commissioner office. Hours vary by county.

Source: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Can I vote by mail?

Yes. Any registered voter in Nebraska can vote by mail, and nobody asks you why.

  • Ask for your ballot by: For this one, go by the state's own page: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections.
  • Send it back by: For this one, go by the state's own page: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections.

Ask for your ballot on the state's own form.

Source: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

What do I bring?

Bring a photo ID. Nebraska requires voters to show an acceptable photo ID in every election, with a few exceptions.

Source: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

Where and when on November 3?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.

  • Polls are open: 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Central Time, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Mountain Time.
  • Where you vote: find your polling place on the state's own lookup.

Source: Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections. Checked October 11, 2026.

The last word

Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections has it, not me. If anything here differs from Nebraska Secretary of State, Elections, the state is right. I checked these pages on October 11, 2026, and rules can change after that.

I tell you how to vote. I never tell you who to vote for. See who is on the ballot in Nebraska, or ask the others in Nebraska how voting went for them.
